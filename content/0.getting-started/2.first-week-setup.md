Work through these steps in order. Each one depends on the step before it: prices are set per membership, memberships need courts, and online payments need your company details.

::steps{level="3"}

### Fill in your club's details

In **Club Admin → General**, upload your logo, check the club name and choose the club's language. Under **Club status**, pick the **Sports** you offer and your **Club type** (Club, Academy or League). Add your contact details, social links and address.

[General →](/interface/settings#general)

### Add your company details

In **Club Admin → Business → Company info**, enter your legal company name, tax ID and bank account. They appear on invoices and are needed to take payments. Check **Currency** and **Timezone** while you're there.

[Company info →](/interface/settings#company-info)

### Add your locations (only if you have more than one site)

If the club runs more than one site, add each one under **Club Admin → Locations** and put each court at its site. A club with one site can skip this step: it is shown at its own address.

[Locations →](/interface/settings#locations)

### Set up your courts

Courts are created in the member app: open the **App** tab, then **Court management**. For each court, set its name, sport, surface, whether it is indoor, and how long a booking is.

[Courts →](/interface/courts)

### Create your memberships

Every booking is priced by membership, so create your plans before you set prices. Every club needs a **basic membership**, which everyone without another plan gets — usually called "Guest" or "Non-member". Then add your paid plans, such as Adult, Junior and Senior.

[Memberships →](/interface/memberships)

### Set court prices

For each court, open **Prices** and enter the price per membership and time of day. If you also want a printed price list on the booking page, build it under **Settings → Price list**. That list is for display only and doesn't change what bookings cost.

[Court prices →](/interface/courts#prices)

### Decide your booking rules

**Settings → Reservations** decides who can book (for example, whether non-members can) and how: single or partner bookings, the waitlist, a two-hour limit. How far ahead each member can book and cancel is set per membership.

[Booking rules →](/interface/settings/booking-rules)

### Connect payments

Choose a provider under **Business → Payment gateway** and complete its setup. For most clubs that's **Stripe**. Then turn on **Enable online payment** under **Settings → Payments**.

[Payments →](/interface/payments)

### Publish your terms and sign-up rules

Create your terms and privacy policy as website pages, then pick them under **Settings → Legal**. Under **Settings → Signup**, decide whether new users need your approval.

[Sign-up and legal →](/interface/settings/signup-and-legal)

### Invite your staff

Add each person who runs the club under **Members → Administrators**. They get an email invitation if they don't have a SportyPlus account yet.

[Administrators and roles →](/interface/settings/administrators-and-roles)

::

When you've finished, open the **Website** tab and book a court yourself, as a member would. That is the quickest way to find a missing price or a court that isn't bookable.
