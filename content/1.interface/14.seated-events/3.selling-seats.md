Seats are set up on the event itself. Open the event under **Website → Events → Events**, and scroll down to **Sell event**.

## Switch the event to numbered seats

An event without products of its own shows two choices: **Open admission** and **Numbered seats**. Choose **Numbered seats**.

::note
An event is sold either by its own products or by seat, never both. If the event already has products, delete them first.
::

Choose the **seating plan** of the court or hall where the event is held, then **Use this plan**. The list shows your own plans and those of partner clubs that let you sell seated events at their venue.

The event gets its own copy of the plan, and moves to the plan's location. Its zones appear next to a preview of the map.

Until the first seat is sold or held in a checkout, you can:

- **Apply the plan again**, to pick up changes made to the plan since;
- **Change plan**, to seat the event from another plan;
- go **Back to open admission**.

After that, the plan is fixed for the event. Ticket types and prices stay editable.

## Ticket types

A ticket type is who a ticket is for: *Adult*, *Child under 12*, *Member*. Choose **Add ticket type** for each one, and set:

![Ticket types](/images/event-ticket-types.jpg)

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | What buyers see, such as *Adult*. |
| **Who may buy it** | **Anyone**; **By age**, with an age range that is **checked at the door**; or **Members**, with the memberships that qualify, **checked at checkout** against the person the ticket is for. |
| **Per full-price ticket** | The most of this type allowed per full-price ticket in one order, for example 2 children per adult. Leave it empty for no limit. |
| **Shown at the door** | A short word the door screen shows in large type, such as *CHILD*, so the steward knows what to check. |

Where the venue allows children on a parent's lap, switch on **Children up to … may sit on a parent's lap** and choose **Add lap ticket**. A lap ticket takes no seat. It is bought together with a seated ticket, at most one per seat, and it has its own QR code.

::tip
To reuse a set of ticket types, type a name under **Save these as…** and save. On your next event, **Use saved ticket types** fills them in.
::

## Prices

The price table has a row per zone and a column per ticket type. For each cell:

![Prices per zone and ticket type](/images/event-ticket-prices.jpg)

- enter a price;
- enter **0** to make it free, for example children free in every zone;
- or leave it **empty** where that ticket type isn't sold in that zone.

The menu on each column header fills the whole column with **Free in every zone** or **Not offered anywhere**.

Choose **Save ticket prices**. Each priced cell becomes a ticket product. These products are kept out of your shop and product lists, and they appear on orders and invoices by name, for example *Padel Open – finals day · VIP · Adult*.

## What buyers see

On the event's page on your website, a seated event shows **Choose seats** instead of a list of tickets. Buyers sign in, then:

![Choosing seats on the club website](/images/event-seat-picker.jpg)

1. Tap free seats on the map. Taken seats are grey. For each seat they choose the ticket type and, where allowed, tick **A child up to … on the lap**.
2. For standing areas, they choose how many places of each ticket type.
3. Or they use **Find me the best seats**: they pick a zone and how many tickets of each type, and SportyPlus finds seats side by side, as near the front and the middle as possible.
4. **Continue to payment** holds the seats for 30 minutes and opens the usual payment page.

If someone takes a seat while they are choosing, the map refreshes and that seat drops out of their choice. If their choice leaves one free seat stranded between taken ones, they see a gentle warning, but they can still buy.

Their tickets, in the member app and on the website, show the section, row and seat, and so do the Apple Wallet, Google Wallet and Samsung Wallet passes.

The member app has the same seat picker. Staff can also sell seats at the till: see [At the door and the till](/interface/seated-events/on-sale#at-the-door-and-the-till).

## Who can buy what

- **Member prices** are checked at checkout against the person each ticket is for. A member can give a member-priced ticket to someone who isn't a member: they are told it is priced for members, but they can still give it.
- **Ages** are checked at the door, not at checkout. The door screen shows the ticket type in large type.
- **Limits per full-price ticket** are checked when the seats are chosen.
