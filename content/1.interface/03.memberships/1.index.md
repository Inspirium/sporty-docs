A membership plan decides how a member uses your club: how far ahead they can book, how late they can cancel, which courts they can book, and how many free hours they get. Court prices are also set per plan (see [Court prices](/interface/courts#prices)).

Manage plans under **Club Admin → Community → Memberships → Memberships**.

## The plan list

![Membership plans](/images/memberships-list.jpg)

Each card shows a plan's main rules and its price, or **Free**. Drag a card by its handle (☰) to change the order plans are shown to members, then save the order.

### Basic membership

The row of names at the top picks your **basic membership**: the plan everyone without another membership gets. Every new account in your club starts on it. Most clubs name it "Guest" or "Non-member" and leave it free.

Click a name to make that plan the basic one. A club has exactly one basic membership.

## Creating a plan

Choose **Create new**.

![New membership](/images/memberships-new.jpg)

1. Enter a **Title**, a short **Description**, and, under **Content**, the full description members read before they buy.
2. Leave **Public membership** on to show the plan to everyone. Switch it off for a plan you only give out yourself, such as one for staff or coaches.
3. Switch on **Family membership** for a plan that covers a whole family, and set the **Max number of family members**. You can only choose this when you create the plan.
4. Choose **Save and continue**.

The plan's edit page opens, where you set its rules and price.

## Booking rules

On the edit page, scroll to **Membership features → Reservation management**. Each card is one rule. Click a card to set it.

![Membership features](/images/memberships-features.jpg)

- **Booking range** — how many days ahead a member can book, from none (0) up to 360 days, or a number of your choice.
- **Booking policy** — whether the member must add the other players when they book.
- **Cancellation policy** — how many hours before the start a member can still cancel. **0** means members can't cancel. **−1** lets them cancel up to an hour *after* the booking started, which is useful if rain stops play.
- **Max bookings per user** — how many bookings, in hours, a member can hold at once. Choose **No limit** to allow any number.
- **Payment** — whether the member must pay by card when they book, or may pay at the club.
- **Included hours** — free court time each month, such as four hours a month. Choose whether unused time is lost at the end of the month (**Reset every month**) or carried over. You can limit the free hours to selected courts.
- **Included courts** — the courts this plan may book. If you limit it to some courts, **Show excluded courts** shows the others on the booking page without letting members book them.
- **Minimal minutes before booking** — how close to the start time a booking can still be made, such as 15 or 30 minutes.

Choose **Save** at the bottom of the page.

## Selling a plan

A plan is sold through a **sellable product** attached to it. Under **Sell membership**, choose **Create sellable item**, or **Add new** if the plan already has one:

- **Name**, **Description** and **Price**.
- **Available quantity** — how many can be sold.
- **Type**:
  - **Single purchase** — the member pays once.
  - **Subscription** — the member is charged again every interval. Set the **Subscription interval** (day, week, month or year) and how many intervals there are between charges.

For a subscription you can also:

- Choose when it starts: immediately, at the beginning of this period, or at the beginning of the next. If it doesn't start immediately, you can prorate the first charge.
- Add a **Joining fee**, by picking an existing shop product.
- Take a **Security deposit**.

Choose **Save product**. A plan can have several products, for example a monthly and a yearly price.

Members can then buy the plan from your website or the app. Online payment needs a connected [payment gateway](/interface/payments).

## Changing or deleting a plan

Open a plan to change its title, content, rules or products, and choose **Save**. **Delete membership**, at the bottom, removes the plan.

To change one member's membership instead, for example to extend it, see [Changing a member's membership](/interface/members#changing-a-members-membership).
