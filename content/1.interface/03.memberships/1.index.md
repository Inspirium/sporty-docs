Membership plans define the pricing and booking rules a member gets once they subscribe — how far ahead they can reserve a court, how late they can cancel, and whether they're limited in how many active reservations they can hold.

Manage plans from **Club Admin → Community → Memberships**.

## Membership plans

Each plan can define:

- **Reservation span** — how many days in advance a member can book
- **Cancelation range** — how close to the start time a member can still cancel
- **Limit on total active reservations** — cap on simultaneous bookings
- **Included products** — shop products bundled with the plan
- **Price** — free, or a fixed price with a payment interval (e.g. yearly)

![Membership plan list](/images/memberships-list.jpg)

Use **Create new** to add a plan, or open an existing card with **More** to edit it.

## Basic membership

At the top of the page you can pick a **basic membership** — the plan automatically assigned to users who don't hold any other membership. This is typically a free "Guest" tier.

## Membership settings

**Memberships → Membership settings** controls plan-wide behavior, such as whether the module is shown on the public website.
