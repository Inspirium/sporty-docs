SportyPlus handles payments for reservations, memberships, shop orders, and academy fees through a payment gateway you connect once for the whole club.

## Payment gateways

Go to **Club Admin → Business → Payment gateway** to connect a provider:

- **Stripe Express** — card payments, with a test mode for trying things out before going live
- **PayFast** — card payments
- **Bank transfer** — customers pay via manual bank transfer
- **E-wallet** — pay from an internal club wallet/credit balance

![Payment gateway selection](/images/payments-gateways.jpg)

Each gateway can be activated independently and has its own **payment fee** percentage, applied on top of prices at checkout.

## Orders

**Club Admin → Commercial → Orders** lists every order placed in your club — shop purchases, membership subscriptions, event tickets, and academy fees.

![Order history](/images/orders-list.jpg)

From an order you can view details, send a copy by email, send a payment reminder, or cancel it.

- **Invoices** — generated invoices for completed orders
- **Refunds** — process a refund for a completed order
- **New order** — create an order manually, e.g. for a walk-in payment
