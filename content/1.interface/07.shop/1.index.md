The Shop is your club's product catalog. A "product" isn't just merchandise — it's anything a customer can buy, grouped into four categories:

- **Shop** — physical goods (equipment, apparel, balls...)
- **Membership** — membership plans sold as purchasable products
- **Event** — tickets for events
- **Academy** — academy program fees

Manage products from **Club Admin → Commercial → Shop → Products**.

![Product list](/images/shop-products.jpg)

## Creating a product

Use **Create new** to add a product. Set its name, price, category, and (for subscription-type products) a recurring billing interval — subscription products are labeled accordingly in the list.

## Categories

**Shop → Categories** lets you group shop products so customers can browse by category on the public shop page.

## Orders

Every purchase — whether shop, membership, event, or academy — becomes an order under [Orders](/interface/payments#orders).
