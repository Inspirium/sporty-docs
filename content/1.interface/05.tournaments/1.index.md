Tournaments are elimination-bracket events. They're created from the same [Leagues](/interface/leagues) module — when creating a new league, choose the **Cup / Tournament** type.

![Selecting the Cup / Tournament type](/images/leagues-type-selector.jpg)

## How it works

A Cup / Tournament league seeds players or teams into a single-elimination bracket. As matches are recorded, winners automatically advance to the next round until a champion is determined.

Use the same league tools to run a tournament:

- **Groups** — seed players/teams into the bracket
- **Matches** — record match results round by round
- **Standings** — see who has advanced and who's been eliminated

## Tournament status

Like any league, a tournament moves through statuses — **Draft** while you're setting it up, **Open** while registration is available, **Active** once matches are underway, and **Finished** once a winner is decided.
