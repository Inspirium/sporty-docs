## Sign-up options

**Club Admin → Settings → Signup**

![Sign-up options](/images/settings-signup.jpg)

### User validation

**Validate users** makes every new sign-up wait for your approval. Until you approve them, they can't book or buy anything at your club. Use it for private clubs, and leave it off if anyone may play.

### Asking people to join

**Require user to join club** shows a message to visitors who have a SportyPlus account but haven't joined your club yet. When it's on, write the **Signup message heading** and **Signup message**.

### Membership purchase

**Enable membership purchase** offers a membership during sign-up. Write the heading and message to show with it.

Press **Save**.

## Legal pages

**Club Admin → Settings → Legal** tells SportyPlus which of your website pages are your terms and conditions and your privacy policy. They are linked at registration, at checkout, and in other places members agree to them.

![Legal pages](/images/settings-legal.jpg)

1. Create the two pages first, under **Website → Pages** (see [Pages](/website/pages)). If a page doesn't exist yet, the **Create page here** link under each field takes you there.
2. Come back and pick each page from its list.
3. **Save**.
