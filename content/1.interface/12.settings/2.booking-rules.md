Booking rules are set at two levels:

- **Club-wide**, under **Settings → Reservations**, covered on this page.
- **Per membership**, on each [membership plan](/interface/memberships#booking-rules): how far ahead a member can book, how late they can cancel, and how many bookings they can hold.

Prices are set **per court**, under [court prices](/interface/courts#prices). The **Price list** on this page only publishes them.

League clubs have no courts, so they don't see either screen.

## Reservation settings

**Club Admin → Settings → Reservations**

![Reservation settings](/images/settings-reservations.jpg)

### Booking access

- **Allow non-member booking** — people without a membership can book courts. Switch it off to keep the courts for members.

### Booking options

- **Show booking notice** — shows a notice on the booking page, for example about court maintenance.
- **Enable waitlist** — when the courts are fully booked, members can join a waitlist.
- **Single booking only** — a reservation is for one person, so members can't add a partner.
- **2-hour booking limit** — no reservation can last longer than two hours.
- **External booking indicator** — marks bookings made outside your club's app, such as through a partner booking site, on the booking grid.

### Membership options

- **Multiple memberships** — lets a member hold more than one membership at once, such as a court membership and a school membership.
- **Show user membership** — shows each member's membership on the booking page.

Press **Save** at the bottom of the page.

## Price list

**Club Admin → Settings → Price list** is the price list members open with **Show price list** on the booking page.

![Price list editor](/images/settings-price-list.jpg)

::warning
The price list is **for display only**. What a booking costs comes from each [court's prices](/interface/courts#prices). If you change a rate, change it on the court too, or members will be charged a different price from the one they read.
::

Use it to publish what's hard to read from the booking grid: morning and evening rates, weekend prices, a lighting or heating supplement.

1. Choose **Add column**. A column is one block of rates, such as "Morning" or "Weekend".
2. Give the column a **Heading** and, optionally, the **Hours** it applies to, such as "08:00–16:00".
3. Add a rate for each line, such as "1 hour" and its amount. Choose **Add rate** for more lines.
4. Add more columns if you need them, and reorder them with the arrows.
5. **Save**. Save stays disabled until every column has a heading and at least one rate.

### Price list rules

Croatian price-list rules require a reduced price to be shown next to the price it was reduced from. If they apply to you:

- Switch on **Show the anchor price**, and set the **Reference date** whose prices count as the original ones.
- **Outlet form** and **Outlet code** name your business on the published list. Leave them empty to use the defaults: a generic description, and your club's subdomain.

The rules have their own **Save** button.
