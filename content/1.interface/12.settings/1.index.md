Club-wide configuration lives under **Club Admin → Club**.

## General

**Club Admin → General** holds your club's identity: name, logo, subdomain/domain, description, language, contact details, socials, and address. It also sets the **club type** (Classic, Academy, or Fitness), which determines whether modules like the [Academy Manager](/interface/schools) are shown, and whether **access management** (hardware integrations) is enabled.

## Business

- **Company info** — legal/billing details for your organization
- **Payment gateway** — see [Payments](/interface/payments)
- **Integrations** — hardware and third-party integrations (see [Courts → Integrations](/interface/courts/integrations))

## Signup

**Settings → Signup** controls how new users join your club:

- **Validate users** — require admin approval before new users can interact with the club (approvals show up under [Members → Pending requests](/interface/members))
- **Require user to join club** — force a join step before booking
- **Membership purchase options** — customize the message and call-to-action shown during signup

![Signup settings](/images/settings-signup.jpg)

## Legal

**Settings → Legal** lets you pick which of your [website pages](/website) serve as your **Terms and conditions** and **Privacy policy**, shown during registration and elsewhere.

![Legal pages settings](/images/settings-legal.jpg)

## Display, Invoices, Roles

- **Display** — visual/display preferences for the club interface
- **Invoices** — invoicing configuration for orders
- **Roles** — staff roles and permissions, controlling what each admin/cashier/trainer account can access
