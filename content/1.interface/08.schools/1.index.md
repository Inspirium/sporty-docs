The Academy Manager runs your club's tennis school: training programs, students, trainers, billing, and equipment. Enable it by setting your **Club type** to **Academy** in **Club Admin → General**, then manage everything under **Club Admin → Academy Manager**.

## Dashboard

The dashboard gives a quick overview of active programs, students, pending invoices, and expired documents, with shortcuts into each area.

![Academy Manager dashboard](/images/academy-dashboard.jpg)

## Programs and schedule

**Programs** are the groups students train in — each has its own schedule, price, and training timetable.

![Program list with revenue](/images/academy-programs.jpg)

Use **Schedule** to see training sessions across all programs at a glance.

## Students and trainers

- **Students** — manage student profiles, subscriptions, and status
- **Trainers** — manage coach accounts assigned to programs

## Finance

- **Coach hours** — log hours worked per coach and program, used for payroll
- **Billing** — track invoices, payments, and send payment reminders to parents/students

## Resources

- **Medical** — track medical certificates, their expiry dates, and document statuses
- **Equipment** — assign equipment to students and track when it's returned
