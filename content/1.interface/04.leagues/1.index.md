The Leagues module runs any kind of organized competition — round-robin leagues, rating-based ladders, pyramids, American-format events, or elimination tournaments (see [Tournaments](/interface/tournaments)).

Manage leagues from **Club Admin → Leagues**.

![League list](/images/leagues-list.jpg)

## League types

When you create a league, pick the format that matches your competition:

| Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Standard League** | Classic league with groups and a round-robin format |
| **Elo League** | Ranking based on an Elo rating system |
| **Padel League** | League for padel with pairs, tracking left/right positions |
| **Pyramid** | Pyramidal competition system, players challenge up the ladder |
| **Americanino** | American-format competition with rotating partners, for 4 to 24 players. When the count doesn't divide into fours, players sit out in turn |
| **Mexicano** | Rotating partners, with each round paired from the standings so far. Its games are drawn one round at a time |
| **Cup / Tournament** | Elimination tournament played out as a bracket — see [Tournaments](/interface/tournaments) |

![League type selector](/images/leagues-type-selector.jpg)

## Running a league

Once a league is created you can manage it through its own tabs:

- **Groups** — organize players/teams into groups
- **Matches** — schedule and record match results
- **Standings** — automatically calculated rankings based on results

A league also has a status (Draft, Open, Active, Finished) that controls whether players can join and whether matches can be recorded.

## Waiting lists and leagues that repeat

A league takes sign-ups only while it has places and its sign-up is open. When it is full or closed, the member app says so and doesn't put players on a waiting list. If your club runs planned competitions that are taking sign-ups, the app points players to them instead.

For a fixed number of places with a waiting list, or a league that runs every week or every few weeks, set it up as a [planned competition](/interface/leagues/planned-competitions). The league you've built becomes its template. On a league that hasn't started, the **Settings** tab offers **Create a planned competition** to do this in one step.

## Printing the schedule

To print a league's schedule as a PDF, open the league in the member app and choose **Print schedule**. It can be printed **By group**, **By day**, or as **Upcoming matches only**. Americano and mexicano groups are printed round by round, with the pairs in each match and who sits out each round.
