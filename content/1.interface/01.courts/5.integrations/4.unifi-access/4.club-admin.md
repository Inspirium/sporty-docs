Everything for UniFi Access lives on one page: **Club Admin → Business → Integrations → UniFi
Access** (`/admin/app/business/integrations/unifi-access`). You need to be an admin of the club.

Before you start, the [VPN](/interface/courts/integrations/unifi-access/vpn) should be connected
and you should have an [API token](/interface/courts/integrations/unifi-access/api-token).

## Console

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | on |
| **Console address** | `https://` + the console's LAN address + `:12445`, for example `https://192.168.18.1:12445` |
| **API token** | the token you copied from UniFi Access |

Press **Save**.

The console address must be exactly that shape: `https`, a private network address (`10.x.x.x`,
`172.16–31.x.x` or `192.168.x.x`), and port `12445`. Anything else is refused on save. That's
deliberate: SportyPlus only ever talks to a console through your tunnel.

The token can't be read back once it's saved. The field shows **Saved. Leave blank to keep it.**
Leave it empty to keep the stored token, or paste a new one to replace it.

## Doors

Press **Read doors from console**. SportyPlus lists every door your console has, with its name and
full location, and shows when the doors were last read.

For each door, choose the **Courts** it serves:

- one or more courts, for a door that only leads to those courts
- **All courts**, for a door every booking passes through, such as the building entrance.
  Choosing it clears any individual courts, and choosing a court clears **All courts**.
- nothing, for a door SportyPlus should never give access to, such as an office or a store room

Press **Save** at the bottom of the list to keep the courts you chose.

Reading the doors again later keeps the courts you already set. A door that has been removed from
the console disappears from the list, along with its courts.

## Unlocking a door

Every door has an **Unlock** button. It opens the door **once, immediately**, for whoever is
standing at it. You're asked to confirm first.

The console's own log records the unlock under your name, prefixed `SportyPlus:`, so you can
always see who opened a door from Club Admin. A few unlocks a minute is the limit.

## Door PINs

This section is where bookings turn into door access.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Issue door PINs for bookings** | turns door PINs on for your club | **off** |
| **Minutes before the booking** | how early a PIN starts working | 15 |
| **Minutes after the booking** | how long it keeps working once the booking ends | 15 |
| **PIN length** | the length of new PINs, which must match the PIN length set on your console | 6 |

Nothing happens until you turn **Issue door PINs for bookings** on and save. So you can connect the
console and match doors first, and switch PINs on when you're ready, ideally with someone at the
club to try a door.

### What members get

Once PINs are on, within a minute of a booking being paid:

- **every player and every coach** on the booking can get in with their own PIN. People only
  watching a booking don't get one.
- the PIN works on the doors that serve the booked court, plus any **All courts** door
- from the chosen minutes before the booking starts until the chosen minutes after it ends

A member's PIN is their **entry code**, which the app and booking reminder emails already show. It
stays the same from booking to booking. A member who already has an entry code from Weblock keeps
that code. Otherwise SportyPlus creates one of the PIN length you set.

A booking that is **still waiting for payment** gets no access until it's paid. A **cancelled**
booking stops working within a minute.

### How it looks on the console

Each member appears in UniFi Access as **one visitor**, named after them, carrying their PIN.
SportyPlus moves that visitor from booking to booking: once one booking's time has passed, the
visitor's time and doors change to the member's next booking. Two bookings back to back become one
continuous stretch, so the member isn't locked out in between. When a member has nothing else
booked, their visitor is removed, but they keep their PIN for next time.

Don't edit these visitors on the console. SportyPlus corrects them within a minute.

::note
Turning **Issue door PINs for bookings** off stops SportyPlus from creating or changing visitors,
but it doesn't remove the ones already on the console. Delete them in UniFi Access if you need
access to stop immediately.
::

## When something goes wrong

SportyPlus shows the console's answer when an action fails.

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| *The UniFi Access console could not be reached* | the VPN is down, or the [firewall rule](/interface/courts/integrations/unifi-access/vpn#_4-allow-sportyplus-through-to-unifi-access) is missing. Check that the SportyPlus VPN client is on. |
| *refused the request (401)* | the API token is wrong or has expired. Create a new one and paste it in. |
| *refused the request (403)* | the token is missing a [permission](/interface/courts/integrations/unifi-access/api-token#permissions) |
| *The console address … is not allowed* | the address isn't in the form `https://192.168.x.x:12445` |
| *UniFi Access is not set up for this club* | save the console address and the token first |

If a member's PIN doesn't open a door, check that the booking is paid, that its court is matched
to that door, and that **Issue door PINs for bookings** is on. A console that was offline catches
up on its own within a minute of coming back.
