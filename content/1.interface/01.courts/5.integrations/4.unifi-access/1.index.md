If your club's doors run on **UniFi Access** (Ubiquiti's door access system), SportyPlus can
drive them from your bookings:

- every player on a **paid booking** gets a **door PIN** that works only on the doors to their
  court, and only around the time they booked
- club staff can **unlock a door** from Club Admin
- an entrance door can serve **all courts**, so everyone with a booking gets through it

The PIN is the member's **entry code** — the same code your club already shows in the app and in
booking reminder emails. Members don't need to learn a new code for each booking.

## What you need

- A UniFi console running **UniFi Access**, such as a UDM-Pro or a Cloud Key, with your door hubs
  and readers adopted and working. SportyPlus was tested with UniFi OS 5.1, UniFi Access 4.3 and
  UniFi Network 10.6.
- **Owner or admin access** to that console, both to set up a VPN client in UniFi Network and to
  create an API token in UniFi Access.
- A keypad reader that accepts PINs, such as a G3 Intercom or a G2/G3 Reader Pro, on every door
  members should open with a code.

## How it fits together

UniFi Access only answers on your club's own network. Ubiquiti's cloud does not pass it through,
so SportyPlus can't reach it over the internet the way it reaches Shelly Cloud. Instead,
**your console opens a small VPN tunnel to SportyPlus**:

1. Your console dials out to SportyPlus with **WireGuard**. Nothing is opened on your router, and
   it works even when the console sits behind another router, which is common.
2. Through that tunnel, SportyPlus talks to UniFi Access on your console, and to nothing else on
   your network. Your console's firewall rule allows only that one port.
3. You give SportyPlus an **API token** from UniFi Access. It lets SportyPlus read your doors, add
   visitors with PINs and unlock doors.
4. In Club Admin you connect the console, match each door to the courts it serves, and turn on
   door PINs.

Your console enforces every PIN itself, so a member still gets in with their code even if your
internet, or ours, is down when they arrive.

## Setting it up

Do these in order. Each one needs the one before it.

1. [**Connect the VPN**](/interface/courts/integrations/unifi-access/vpn) — download your club's
   WireGuard file from Club Admin, import it on the console, and allow SportyPlus through its
   firewall.
2. [**Create an API token**](/interface/courts/integrations/unifi-access/api-token) in UniFi Access.
3. [**Set it up in Club Admin**](/interface/courts/integrations/unifi-access/club-admin) — the console
   address, the token, your doors and courts, and door PINs.

::note
You don't need to contact us for any of this. Club Admin creates your club's VPN file and tunnel
address, and SportyPlus's side of the tunnel sets itself up within a minute.
::
