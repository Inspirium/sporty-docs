Courts are the bookable resources in your club — tennis courts, padel courts, pickleball courts, or any other playing surface. Members and guests book them from a live availability calendar on your public website.

## Booking calendar

Every court is listed on your club's `/courts` page with a day-by-day availability grid. Visitors pick a date, see which slots are free on each court, and book directly.

![Public court booking calendar](/images/courts-booking-calendar.jpg)

Courts with hardware access control (see [Integrations](/interface/courts/integrations)) show a badge, such as a surface type or lock status, next to their name.

## Reservation settings

Go to **Club Admin → Settings → Reservations** to control how bookings behave club-wide:

- **Allow non-member booking** — let users without an active membership book courts
- **Show booking notice** — display a custom message on the booking page
- **Enable waitlist** — let members join a waitlist when a court is fully booked
- **Single booking only** — restrict users to one active reservation at a time
- **Multiple memberships** / **Show user membership** — control how membership status interacts with booking

![Reservation settings screen](/images/courts-reservation-settings.jpg)

## Access control and lights

Connect physical access controls and lights to your courts so opening a court and turning on the lights happens automatically when a reservation starts. See [Integrations](/interface/courts/integrations) for supported hardware (Weblock, Shelly, Salto).
