Courts are the bookable resources of your club: tennis, padel and pickleball courts, or any other playing area. Members book them from a live calendar on your website and in the app.

Courts are managed in the member app, not in Club Admin. Open the **App** tab at the top of the admin, then the menu, then **Court management**. You can also go straight to `/app/admin/courts` on your club's address.

## Court management

![Court management](/images/courts-list.jpg)

Each card shows one court:

- Its opening hours and booking length.
- Its price range, and the prices that apply on a sample date.
- Whether it is indoor (Covered) or outdoor (External), and its surface.
- Badges for extras such as **Match recording**.

A red **The court is not active** banner means members can't book it. Drag the handle on the right of a card to change the order courts appear in.

Each card has three buttons:

- **Edit the court** — the court's details and booking options.
- **Prices** — its opening hours and prices.
- **More** — further options.

To add a court, choose **Add new court**.

## Court details

**Edit the court** opens the court's settings. Press the save button at the bottom of the page when you're done.

![Editing a court](/images/courts-edit.jpg)

- **Court name** and a photo.
- **Court in operation** — switch to **Not operational** to close a court, for example during resurfacing. It stays in the list but can't be booked.
- **Members can book this court** — choose **No** for a court only staff can book, such as one kept for the school.
- **Type of court** — outdoor or indoor. **Reflectors** — whether the court has floodlights.
- **Reservations** — the booking length: 30 minutes, a full hour, or 90 minutes.
- **Mandatory 1:30h reservation for users** — members can only book 90-minute slots.
- **Half-hour hole** — whether a booking may leave a 30-minute gap that nobody else can use. Choose **Not possible** to protect your schedule from gaps. You can then:
  - lift the protection a set number of hours before the slot starts, so short gaps can still be filled at the last minute;
  - allow a 30-minute gap just before closing time.
- **Half-hour reservation for members** — whether members may book a single 30-minute slot.
- **Confirmation** — whether bookings on this court must be confirmed by the club.
- **Show the court on the SportyPlus page** — whether the court is listed on sporty.plus, as well as on your own site.
- **Type of court** (the sport) and **Type of substrate** (the surface) — used for filters and the badges on the booking page.

**Delete court**, at the bottom of the page, removes the court.

## Prices

**Prices** is where you set when a court is open and what it costs. A court can only be booked in the hours you have priced, so a slot with no price can't be booked at all.

![Court prices](/images/courts-prices.jpg)

Prices are grouped by [membership](/interface/memberships). For each membership:

1. Choose **Enter prices**.
2. Pick the days of the week, the start and end time, and the price per booking slot.
3. Add as many entries as you need, for example a cheaper morning rate and a dearer evening rate.

Always enter prices for your [basic membership](/interface/memberships#basic-membership): they are what everyone without another membership pays.

To save time:

- **Copy prices from another court** copies another court's opening hours and prices to this court.
- **Seasonal prices** — choose **Edit seasons** to define seasons such as Summer and Winter, each with its own prices. **Show seasonal prices** filters the list by season.

::tip
The [Price list](/interface/settings/booking-rules#price-list) under Club Admin → Settings is a separate, display-only list for the booking page. Change it whenever you change court prices, so the two agree.
::

## The booking calendar

Every court is listed on your website's `/courts` page, with a day-by-day calendar of free and booked slots. Visitors pick a date, see which slots are free on each court, and book.

![Public court booking calendar](/images/courts-booking-calendar.jpg)

To show a preview of the calendar on your homepage, use the Courts module under **Website → Courts**.

Club-wide booking rules, such as whether non-members may book, are in [Booking rules](/interface/settings/booking-rules).

## Access control and lights

You can connect door locks and court lights, so that a court opens and its lights come on when a reservation starts. See [Integrations](/interface/courts/integrations) for the supported hardware.
