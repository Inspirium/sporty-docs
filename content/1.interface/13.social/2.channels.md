A **channel** is one social account you can publish to — for example your club's Facebook Page or its Telegram channel. Connect channels on **Club Admin → Social → Channels**.

There are three ways to connect, depending on the platform:

- **Sign in** — Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and X send you to the provider to authorize SportyPlus, then bring you back.
- **Paste a credential** — Telegram, Discord, and Slack ask you for a bot token or a webhook URL. SportyPlus checks it against the platform straight away.
- **Install an app** — Reddit uses the SportyPlus publisher app, which a subreddit moderator installs.

You can connect one account per platform per club.

## Channel status

Each channel on the **Channels** tab shows one of:

| Status | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Connected | Ready to publish to. |
| Finish setup | Connected, but you still need to choose which account to post as. Select **Choose account**. |
| Reconnect needed | The stored authorization stopped working. Select **Reconnect** and sign in again. |
| Disconnected | Not connected. Select **Connect**. |

## Facebook and Instagram

One Facebook sign-in connects both. Select **Connect** on the Facebook & Instagram card.

Before you start, make sure:

- you are an **admin** of the Facebook Page you want to post to;
- for Instagram, the account is a **Business or Creator** account and is linked to that Facebook Page.

After you authorize SportyPlus:

1. The channel shows **Finish setup**. Select **Choose account**.
2. Pick the **Facebook Page** to post as.
3. For Instagram, pick the **Instagram account** in the same way.

Each becomes its own channel, so you can publish to Facebook, Instagram, or both.

## LinkedIn

Select **Connect** and authorize SportyPlus with the LinkedIn account you use to manage your club's presence.

Then select **Choose account** and pick where posts go:

- your **personal profile**, or
- a **Company Page** you administer.

## X

Select **Connect** and authorize SportyPlus with your club's X account. Posts are published as that account. No further setup is needed.

::warning
Publishing to X uses the X API, which X offers only on **paid plans**. This cost is set and billed by X, not SportyPlus, and it applies on top of your SportyPlus subscription. Check the current plans and prices in the [X API pricing table](https://developer.x.com/#pricing) before you connect X.
::

## Reddit

Reddit posts are published by the **SportyPlus publisher app**, which a subreddit moderator installs into the subreddit.

1. Select **Connect**. SportyPlus shows a **pairing code** and a link to the app.
2. Open the app page and install the app into the subreddit you moderate.
3. During installation, paste the **pairing code** into the app's settings.
4. Back in SportyPlus, select **I've done this — check**. The channel switches to **Connected**.

Notes:

- Posts appear from the app's bot account, not your personal account.
- The subreddit is fixed to where the app is installed. When you compose a post you set the **title**; you don't choose the subreddit.
- The pairing code expires after a short time. If it runs out, select **Connect** again for a new one.

## Telegram

1. In Telegram, open a chat with **@BotFather** and create a bot. BotFather gives you a **bot token**.
2. Add the bot to your channel and make it an **administrator** with permission to post messages.
3. In SportyPlus, select **Connect** on the Telegram card.
4. Paste the **bot token** and the channel's **@username** (or its numeric ID for a private channel).
5. Select **Save**. SportyPlus checks that the bot can reach the channel and connects it.

## Discord

1. In Discord, open **Server Settings → Integrations → Webhooks** and create a webhook for the channel you want to post to.
2. Copy the webhook's **URL**.
3. In SportyPlus, select **Connect** on the Discord card, paste the **webhook URL**, and select **Save**.

## Slack

1. Create an **Incoming Webhook** for the Slack channel you want to post to, from your Slack workspace's app settings.
2. Copy the webhook **URL**.
3. In SportyPlus, select **Connect** on the Slack card, paste the **webhook URL**, optionally add a **channel name** for display, and select **Save**.

## Reconnecting

If a channel shows **Reconnect needed**, its saved authorization was rejected — for example a password change, a revoked app, or an expired token. Select **Reconnect** and repeat the connect steps for that platform. Your post history is kept.

## Disconnecting

Select **Disconnect** to stop publishing to a channel. SportyPlus forgets the stored credentials but keeps the channel's post history.

To fully revoke access on the platform's side as well:

- **Telegram** — remove the bot from the channel, or revoke its token in BotFather.
- **Discord / Slack** — delete the webhook.
- **Reddit** — uninstall the SportyPlus publisher app from the subreddit.
- **Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X** — remove SportyPlus from the connected-apps settings of your account.
