Reports give you insight into how your club is performing — bookings, revenue, and member activity.

Find them under **Club Admin → Reports**.

![Reports overview](/images/reports-overview.jpg)

## Available reports

- **Court Revenue** — revenue breakdown by court, period, and booking type
- **Users with no reservations** — members who haven't booked a court in a given period, useful for re-engagement
- **Stripe payments** / **Card transactions** — all transactions processed through your card gateway
- **Members with active membership** — currently active members and their membership details

The dashboard (**Club Admin → Dashboard**) also shows live at-a-glance stats: today's reservations and revenue, weekly/monthly trends, cancellations, and the busiest courts.
