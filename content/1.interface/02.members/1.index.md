Members are the people who use your club: they sign up on your public website, hold a membership plan, and book courts. Manage them from **Club Admin → Community → Members**.

## Member list

The member list shows every registered user, their current membership plan, and whether auto-renew is enabled for their membership.

![Member list](/images/members-list.jpg)

From the actions column you can:

- **View** a member's profile
- **Edit** their details and membership
- **Remove** a member

Use **Create new** to add a member manually, for example someone who signs up in person.

## Pending requests

If your club requires manual approval for new sign-ups (see [Settings → Signup](/interface/settings)), new registrations appear under **Members → Pending requests** until an admin approves or rejects them.

## Roles and permissions

Staff accounts (admins, cashiers, trainers) are assigned roles under **Club Admin → Settings → Roles**. Each role controls which parts of Club Admin a staff member can access — for example, a cashier can process orders but not change club settings.
