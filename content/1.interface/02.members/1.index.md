Members are the people who use your club. Most sign up themselves on your website or in the app, but you can also add someone by hand, such as a person who joins at the front desk.

Manage them under **Club Admin → Community → Members → Member list**.

## The member list

![Member list](/images/members-list.jpg)

- **Name** — the member's name, with their email underneath.
- **Membership** — their membership, its end date, and "+N" if they hold more than one.
- **Auto renew** — whether that membership renews automatically.

Use **Search** to find someone by name or email. The list shows 15 people per page.

The icons on each row:

- The eye and the pencil both open the member's page.
- The red cross removes the member from your club (see [Removing a member](#removing-a-member)).

## Adding a member

Choose **Create new** on the member list.

![Create an account](/images/members-create.jpg)

1. Fill in the first and last name, country, city, postal code, phone and email. Only the street address is optional.
2. Choose a password for them, and type it again under **Confirm password**.
3. Choose **Create account**. The button stays disabled until every required field is filled in.

The new member is given your club's [basic membership](/interface/memberships#basic-membership), and their page opens.

## A member's page

![A member's page](/images/members-detail.jpg)

The **Info** tab holds the member's details: photo, name, gender, birth date, email, phone, tax ID (OIB), and address. Change what you need and choose **Save** at the bottom of the page.

On the same tab:

- **User membership** lists their memberships, and how long each is valid.
- **Change password** sets a new password for them.
- **Assign role to member** gives them one of your club's [roles](/interface/settings/administrators-and-roles#roles).
- **Remove from club**, at the bottom, takes them out of your club (see [Removing a member](#removing-a-member)).

The **Orders** tab lists everything they have bought from the club.

## Changing a member's membership

On the member's page, choose **Edit membership**.

![A member's memberships](/images/members-membership.jpg)

For each membership they hold, you can:

- **Edit expiration date** — change when it starts and ends, for example to extend a membership as a goodwill gesture.
- **Remove** — take the membership away.

To give them another membership, for example one they paid for at the desk:

1. Under **Add new membership**, pick the plan.
2. Switch on **Unlimited membership** for one that never expires, or set its start and end dates.
3. Choose **Assign membership**. The button stays disabled until you pick a plan.

A member can't hold the same plan twice. Members usually get a paid membership by buying it themselves, on your website or in the app.

## Removing a member

The red cross on the member list, or **Remove from club** on a member's page, takes someone out of your club. To remove several people at once, tick them and choose **Delete selected**.

![Removing a member](/images/members-remove.jpg)

Their memberships at your club end, and they can no longer book here. Their SportyPlus account stays, along with any other clubs they belong to. They can join your club again later.

Removing someone doesn't change their staff access. To take that away, remove them under [Administrators](/interface/settings/administrators-and-roles#adding-an-administrator).

People delete their own SportyPlus account themselves, from **My profile**.

## Approving new members

If your club approves sign-ups by hand (**Settings → Signup → Validate users**), new people wait in **Members → Pending requests** until you let them in. Until then they can't book.

![Pending requests](/images/members-pending.jpg)

- **Approve** makes them a member and gives them your club's [basic membership](/interface/memberships#basic-membership). If you have a "user joined the club" email template, they receive it.
- **Reject** turns the request down, after you confirm. They can't book at your club, but their SportyPlus account stays, and they can ask again.
