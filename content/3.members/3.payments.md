Prices, payment methods and what each membership includes are set by your club. The app only shows what your club offers.

## Memberships

**Memberships** (from the home page or the club's menu) lists the memberships your club sells. Each card shows what it includes, for example:

- the price, a **Trial period**, a **Registration fee** or **Deposit**;
- how many days ahead you can book, how late you can cancel, and how many active bookings you can hold;
- a member discount, access hours, and whether you pay for bookings online;
- for family memberships, how many people it covers.

Open a membership to see its full description and its **Payment options** — for example monthly or yearly, with any **Initial fee** or **Security deposit** and what is **Due today**.

### Buying a membership

1. Choose **Buy** on the payment option you want. (If there is no Buy button, your club sells it at the club, not in the app.)
2. Choose how to pay: **Card** or **Wallet**.
3. Under **For**, choose yourself (**Me**) or a family member. A family membership lets you tick the others it **Also covers**.
4. Check the **Total price** and choose **Pay**.

When it's done you'll see **You have successfully activated your membership.** Your membership and its dates appear on the home page under **Your membership**.

Some memberships include **free minutes** for booking courts. Your balance is shown on your profile (**Available free minutes**) and you spend them by choosing **Credits** when you pay for a booking.

When a membership ends, you usually go back to the club's basic membership. Renewing, changing or cancelling a membership is done with the club.

## Wallet

Many clubs give members a prepaid **wallet** (older versions of the app call it **eInvoice**). You top it up and then pay for bookings, memberships and shop items from it. Refunds for cancelled bookings also go back into it.

- **Balance and history:** your wallet balance is on the home page and on your profile under **Finance**. Tap it to see every transaction, filtered by **Top-ups** and **Payments**, with the balance before each one.
- **Top up:** choose **Deposit into account**, enter the amount (the club may set a minimum), add a note if you like, and pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay or your card.
- **Several clubs:** each club has its own wallet. Money in one club's wallet can't be spent at another.

If the app says **You don't have a wallet at this club.**, your club doesn't use wallets or hasn't opened one for you — ask the club.

## Saved card

You can save one card and use it at every SportyPlus club. Go to your picture menu → **Connected accounts** → **Saved card** → **Add**.

- The saved card is used for court bookings, wallet top-ups and paying your share of a booking.
- **Change** replaces it; **Remove** deletes it (you'll then enter a card the next time you pay by card).
- Your bank may ask you to confirm a payment (3-D Secure); confirm it when the app asks.
- Apple Pay and Google Pay can be used for single payments but can't be saved as the card.

## Invoices

Your invoices are on your profile under **Finance** → **Invoices**. You can filter them by date and payment type. Each shows whether it is **Paid**, **Unpaid** or a **Draft**. Open one to see the details and choose **Download PDF invoice**.

Invoices are also emailed to you after you pay.

## The club shop

If your club sells products in the app — balls, rackets, tickets, rentals — they appear under **Products** on the home page.

1. Open a product and choose **Buy** (**Sold out** means none are left).
2. Choose the quantity, and who it is **For** if you are buying for a family member.
3. Pay by card or from your wallet.

Your purchases are under **My orders** in your picture menu. For a paid membership or ticket you bought for your family, **Who is it for?** lets you hand it to yourself, a child or your partner.

## Paying for your family

Parents and partners can pay for each other. See [Family and children](/members/family#paying-for-your-family).
