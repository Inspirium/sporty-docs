## Leagues

**Leagues** in the menu lists your club's leagues, with **My leagues** for the ones you play in. Filter by **Open**, **In progress** or **Completed**, or search. Each league shows its dates, price and status — **Registrations**, **Active**, **Draw** or **Completed**.

### Signing up

Open a league whose registrations are open:

- A free league: choose **Sign up**.
- A paid league: choose **Pay and sign up** and pay by card. If your club collects the fee at the club instead, you just sign up.
- Doubles: pick the pair you're entering with. No pair yet? **Make your first pair** first.
- Padel leagues ask which side you play (**Position**).

You'll see **You are registered in the league.** Until registrations close you can change your mind with **Withdraw** (older versions of the app label this button **Log out**).

If the league is full or its registrations have closed, you can't sign up any more. The app tells you **This league is full.** or **Registration for this league is closed.** If the club runs competitions with a waiting list, it offers **Join a competition with a waiting list**: see [Competitions with a waiting list](#competitions-with-a-waiting-list).

The **Info** tab shows the price, how many players can enter, the registration deadline, start and end dates, and how to contact the organiser.

### Playing your matches

In a running league you see your group, the table and **Results**. On each of your matches you can:

- **Book a court** for it;
- **Enter the result** after you play;
- under **Match options**, record a **Retirement** or **Cancel match**.

Group players also get a **Messages** tab to arrange matches.

In an americano or mexicano, **My court and rotation** shows your group, your court and when you start, then each round: who you play with, who you play against, or **You sit out**. The group also lists who sits out each round.

To print the schedule, choose **Print schedule**: **By group**, **By day**, or **Upcoming matches only**. Americano and mexicano groups are printed round by round, with who sits out.

Ladders with promotion and relegation are a league format, so they work the same way.

## Tournaments

**Tournaments** works like leagues: find one, sign up (**Sign up** or **Pay and sign up**, with any **Entry fee**), and follow the draw. On your match you can **Book a court** and **Enter the result**; **Waiting for the opponent** means the other player isn't known yet.

A full tournament, or one whose registrations have closed, takes no more sign-ups either.

## Competitions with a waiting list

Some clubs run competitions that repeat, such as a weekly americano or league rounds every few weeks. Each has a fixed number of places and a free waiting list. Each date the competition plays is a **round**: an ordinary league or tournament that you play in the usual way.

You reach a competition from a notification, from a full league's **Join a competition with a waiting list**, or from **Open the competition** under a league's overall ranking. A competition taking sign-ups can also have its own page on the club's website.

### Joining

The competition shows how many places are taken, where you stand, and one button:

- **Sign up** — the competition is free. You become a regular if a place is free, otherwise you join the waiting list.
- **Pay the season fee** — a place is free, and the club charges a season fee. You pay once, and the place is yours for the season.
- **Join the waiting list** — all places are taken. The waiting list is free: you pay only when you take a place.
- **Ask to join** — the club approves sign-ups. You're **Awaiting the club's approval** until it decides, and you're told either way. If there's a fee, you pay only once you're approved.

Once you're in, the competition says **You're a regular** or **You're on the waiting list**.

A doubles competition asks which pair you're entering with. Make a pair with your partner first.

Some competitions are for club members only. Every player on the team then needs an active membership at the club, and sometimes a particular type of membership. If you don't have one, the app says **Membership required** and offers **Buy membership**.

To leave, choose **Withdraw**. If you hold a place, you lose it, and it goes to the first person on the waiting list to take it. While you're awaiting approval, **Withdraw request** cancels your request.

### Saying whether you're playing

**Coming up** lists the next rounds. When a new round opens, regulars get a notification. For each round, choose **I'm in** or **I'm out** by the time shown (**Answer by** …).

What happens if you don't answer depends on the competition:

- In some competitions, regulars play unless they say **I'm out**.
- In others, you play only once you say **I'm in**. If you haven't answered, the club may send you a reminder before the deadline. A place nobody confirmed is freed at the deadline.

After the deadline your answer is fixed. A little before the round starts, the round locks and the games are drawn.

### Taking a free place

When a regular gives up their place, everyone on the waiting list gets a push and an email. The first to take the place gets it. If someone was quicker, the app says **Someone was quicker: the place has already been taken.**

- **A place for the whole season** frees up when a regular withdraws before the first round. Take it with **Take the free place**, or pay the season fee with **Take the place and pay {price}**.
- **A place in one round** frees up at the deadline, when a regular isn't playing. It can be taken until the round locks. Take it under **Coming up** with **Take the place**, or **Take it for {price}** when the club charges a fee for single rounds. You play that round only and stay on the waiting list.

A place in a single round is open to any member of the club, not only to the waiting list. If you open the competition, or the round's league, while a place is free, you see **A place is free in this round** and can take it. You play that round only, and you need to belong to the club running the competition.

While you pay, the place is held for you for 15 minutes.

### Notifications

Three notification types cover these competitions: attendance requests and reminders, a free place, and the club's decision on your sign-up. Switch them off under **Notifications** like any other type. Switching off free-place notifications stops both the push and the email.

**Overall ranking** shows the competition's standings.

## Results

**Results** shows **All results**, **My results** and **Live score**.

To record a friendly match, choose **Enter the result**: pick singles or doubles and the players, the surface, then the score. The other side gets a request to **Confirm result** or **Dispute the result**. If they don't answer, the result is confirmed automatically after 24 hours. A disputed result isn't saved and no points change. Results not yet confirmed are marked **Unconfirmed**.

Think a confirmed score is wrong? Open the result and choose **Report a wrong score**.

## Rankings and ratings

**Ranking** shows the club's table, **My position**, and the **Club ELO** and **Global ELO** ratings, with **How are points calculated?** explaining the system. Some clubs show DUPR ratings instead, or no ranking at all.

## DUPR

To link your DUPR account, go to your picture menu → **Connected accounts** → DUPR → **Connect** and sign in to DUPR. Your DUPR ID and ratings then appear there, and your matches are sent to DUPR automatically. **Disconnect** stops that; **Reconnect** appears if DUPR needs you to sign in again.

Some competitions require a linked DUPR account, a rating within a range, or DUPR+. The app tells you when you try to sign up.

## Finding players

- **Find a player!** lists match ads. **Post an ad for a match** to look for players — linked to a booking you have, or as an open ad saying when you're free. You can choose the number of players, skill level, and whether you confirm players yourself. Sign up to someone else's ad from the list.
- **Players** lets you search the club's members and open their profiles, with their rating, wins and **Send a message**.
- **Privacy settings** (from **Edit profile**) control whether your profile is public and which details others see — for all clubs or per club.

## Messages and notifications

- **Messages** in your picture menu holds your conversations. Start one with **New message**; with several recipients it becomes a group. You can send photos and court times.
- **Notifications** lists what the app has sent you. **Receive notifications!** turns on push notifications. Choose **Edit** to switch individual types on or off, per club, for push and email. Invoices, payment requests and password resets are always emailed.
