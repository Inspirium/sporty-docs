Tournaments are knockout events played as a [single-elimination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-elimination_tournament) bracket. Manage them from **Club Admin → Competitions → Tournaments**, and create one with **New tournament**. For how a tournament plays from a player's side, see [How competitions work](/en/members/how-competitions-work#tournaments).

## How it works

Choose a **Bracket size** (4 to 256, or **As many as sign up**). Players or pairs sign up, and when the tournament starts they are drawn into the bracket:

- **Seeds** — give the strongest entrants a seed on the **Players** tab, so they are kept apart in the early rounds ([Seed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seed_%28sports%29)). Everyone else is drawn at random.
- **Byes** — when the entrants don't fill the bracket, some go straight to the second round ([Bye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bye_%28sports%29)).
- As results are recorded, winners move to the next round automatically, and their next opponent is notified.

A tournament has these tabs: **Settings**, **Players**, **Bracket** and **Venues**.

## Tournament status

Like a league, a tournament moves through statuses: **Draft** while you set it up, **Sign-up open** and **Sign-up closed**, **In progress** once matches are underway, and **Finished** once a winner is decided.

## Tournaments that repeat

A tournament takes sign-ups only while it has places and its sign-up is open. When it is full or closed, the member app says so and doesn't put players on a waiting list.

For a tournament that runs regularly, such as a monthly circuit, or finals after a league, set it up as a stage of a [planned competition](/en/interface/leagues/planned-competitions). Each tournament it creates awards points by the round a team reached, and they count towards one table. On a tournament that hasn't started, the **Settings** tab offers **Create a planned competition**, with this tournament as the template.

To print a tournament's schedule as a PDF, open it in the member app and choose **Print schedule**: **By round**, **By day**, or **Upcoming matches only**.
