Tournaments are knockout events. Manage them from **Club Admin → Competitions → Tournaments**, and create one with **New tournament**. For how a tournament plays from a player's side, see [How competitions work](/en/members/how-competitions-work#tournaments).

## How it works

Choose the tournament's **System**:

| System | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Knockout** | One loss and you are out ([single elimination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-elimination_tournament)). |
| **Double elimination** | A first loss drops a player into a losers' bracket; a second loss puts them out. The two brackets' winners meet in a grand final. See [Double elimination and consolation](#double-elimination-and-consolation). |
| **Knockout with consolation** | A knockout, plus a second knockout for everyone who lost their first match, so nobody goes home after one match. |

A change of system takes effect when the bracket is drawn again on the **Bracket** tab.

Choose a **Bracket size** (4 to 256, or **As many as sign up**). Players or pairs sign up, and when the tournament starts they are drawn into the bracket:

- **Seeds** — give the strongest entrants a seed on the **Players** tab, so they are kept apart in the early rounds ([Seed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seed_%28sports%29)). Everyone else is drawn at random.
- **Byes** — when the entrants don't fill the bracket, some go straight to the second round ([Bye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bye_%28sports%29)).
- As results are recorded, winners move to the next round automatically, and their next opponent is notified.

A tournament has these tabs: **Settings**, **Players**, **Bracket** and **Venues**.

## Double elimination and consolation

Both systems draw the whole bracket up front, and players move through it on their own as results are recorded. On the **Bracket** tab, the rounds are shown in parts: **Main draw**, **Losers' bracket**, **Grand final** and **Consolation**.

### Double elimination

- Everyone starts in the main draw. A player who loses there drops into the **Losers' bracket** and plays on. A loss in the losers' bracket puts them out.
- The winner of the main draw and the winner of the losers' bracket meet in the **Grand final**, and its winner wins the tournament. It is played once: a main-draw winner who loses the grand final doesn't get a second match.
- The losers' bracket brings in each round's losers in reverse order, so two players who have just met don't meet again straight away.
- A double-elimination bracket has nearly twice as many matches as a knockout of the same size, so allow the time and courts for it.

### Knockout with consolation

- The main draw is an ordinary knockout, and its final is the tournament's final.
- Everyone who loses their first match plays a consolation knockout. That includes a player who had a bye in the first round and then lost in the second, since that was their first match.
- Players who lose after their first match are out, as in a knockout.

### Byes

When the entrants don't fill the bracket, the first-round byes are handled as in a knockout: use **Advance byes** to move them on. After that, a place in the losers' bracket or the consolation draw that nobody can reach is shown as a **Bye**, and the player waiting there goes through on their own.

::note
Double-elimination and consolation tournaments are run from the club admin for now. Until a later member app release, the app lists the losers' and consolation rounds after the main draw's rounds.
::

## Tournament status

Like a league, a tournament moves through statuses: **Draft** while you set it up, **Sign-up open** and **Sign-up closed**, **In progress** once matches are underway, and **Finished** once a winner is decided.

## Tournaments that repeat

A tournament takes sign-ups only while it has places and its sign-up is open. When it is full or closed, the member app says so and doesn't put players on a waiting list.

For a tournament that runs regularly, such as a monthly circuit, or finals after a league, set it up as a stage of a [planned competition](/en/interface/leagues/planned-competitions). Each tournament it creates awards points by the round a team reached, and they count towards one table. On a tournament that hasn't started, the **Settings** tab offers **Create a planned competition**, with this tournament as the template.

To print a tournament's schedule as a PDF, open it in the member app and choose **Print schedule**: **By round**, **By day**, or **Upcoming matches only**.
