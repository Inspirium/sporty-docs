Staff get into the admin by being made an **administrator** of the club. **Roles** then add or limit specific permissions.

## Adding an administrator

**Club Admin → Community → Members → Administrators**

![Administrators](/images/admins-list.jpg)

1. Under **Add an administrator**, type the email address the person signs in with.
2. Choose **Add**.

The person doesn't need to be a member of the club. Staff who don't play are marked **Not a member** in the list.

If no SportyPlus account uses that email, the person is sent an invitation. They become an administrator as soon as they register from the link in it. Pending invitations are listed with their expiry date: an invitation lasts 14 days, and **Resend** sends it again.

To remove someone's access, choose the bin icon on their row. You can't remove yourself.

::note
Someone who already has the admin open must reload it, or sign out and back in, before newly granted access takes effect.
::

## Roles

**Club Admin → Settings → Roles** lists your club's roles. To create one, choose **Create new**, give the role a **Name**, switch on the permissions it should grant, and **Save**.

![New role](/images/roles-new.jpg)

To give a person a role, open them under **Members → Member list**, choose edit, switch the role on under **Assign role to member**, and **Save**.

::warning
A role doesn't open the admin on its own. The person must also be added as an [administrator](#adding-an-administrator). Roles then grant extra permissions, such as publishing to social media.
::
