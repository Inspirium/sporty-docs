The **Invoices and Stripe fees** report lists every invoice your club issued in a period, from the member app and from the shop. Each invoice shows its payment method, tax, and the fee Stripe kept from the card payment behind it.

Find it under **Club Admin → Reports → Invoices and Stripe fees**.

## Choosing what to export

At the top of the report, pick a **Period** (this month, last month, this year, last year) or set your own **From** and **To** dates. A period can be up to a year long. You can narrow the report further:

- **Payment method**: card, cash, bank transfer or other
- **What was sold**: reservations, wallet top-ups, memberships, subscriptions, shop, card surcharge or other
- **Courts**: only invoices for bookings on the courts you choose

Days follow your club's time zone, so an invoice issued at 00:30 is counted on the day it was issued at the club.

Both downloads use the period and filters you have chosen.

## Download XLS

**Download XLS** gives you a workbook with four sheets:

| Sheet | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Invoices | One row per invoice: number, date, buyer, payment method, net, tax, total, Stripe fee, and its fiscalisation status |
| By day | Takings per day, split by payment method, with Stripe's fees |
| Summary | Totals by payment method, by tax rate and by what was sold |
| Lines | One row per invoice line, for reconciling item by item |

## Export to QuickBooks Online

**QuickBooks (CSV)** downloads a journal file that QuickBooks Online can import. Each day becomes **one journal entry**, numbered `SP-` followed by the date (for example `SP-2026-11-09`). Every entry balances.

| Line in the entry | Debit or credit | Default account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Card payments | Debit | Stripe Clearing |
| Cash payments, bank transfers, other payments | Debit | Undeposited Funds |
| Reservations | Credit (net) | Court Bookings |
| Memberships | Credit (net) | Memberships |
| Subscriptions | Credit (net) | School Fees |
| Shop | Credit (net) | Shop Sales |
| Card surcharge | Credit (net) | Card Surcharges |
| Wallet top-ups | Credit (net) | Member Wallet Balances |
| Other sales | Credit (net) | Other Income |
| Tax collected | Credit | Sales Tax Collected |
| Stripe fees | Debit, and the same amount credited to the card account | Stripe Fees |

Payments are booked with tax included, sales without it, and the tax on its own line. Credit notes and refunds in the period reduce their day's amounts.

::note
**Wallet top-ups are money you owe your members** until they spend it, so by default they go to a liability account, not to income. If your accountant books them differently, point them at another account.
::

Member names and email addresses are not included in the file. Only daily totals go to QuickBooks. The invoice-by-invoice detail stays in SportyPlus and in the XLS download.

### Set your account names

QuickBooks only imports an entry when every account in it already exists in your chart of accounts **under exactly the same name**. Before your first import:

1. Click **QuickBooks (CSV)**.
2. For each line, type the name of the account it should go to in your QuickBooks. Leave a field empty to use the default shown in grey. For a sub-account, write `Parent:Sub-account`, for example `Income:Court Bookings`.
3. Under **Dates in QuickBooks**, pick the date order your QuickBooks uses. If you leave it empty, clubs in the United States get month first (MM/DD/YYYY) and everyone else gets day first (DD/MM/YYYY).
4. Click **Download CSV**.

The account names you enter are remembered in your browser for this club, so you only set them once on each computer.

::tip
Create any accounts you don't have yet before importing: **Stripe Clearing** as a bank or other current asset account, **Member Wallet Balances** as an other current liability account, and **Stripe Fees** as an expense account. Ask your accountant which ones fit your books.
::

### Import the file into QuickBooks

1. In QuickBooks Online, go to **Settings → Import data → Journal entries**.
2. Upload the CSV file and choose the same date format you picked in SportyPlus.
3. Check that the columns are mapped to **Journal No**, **Journal Date**, **Account Name**, **Debits**, **Credits** and **Description**, then import.

A few things to know:

- **Import a month at a time.** QuickBooks may refuse very large files, and a month is usually around 300 lines.
- **Don't import the same days twice.** If QuickBooks warns about duplicate journal numbers, those days are already in your books.
- **Card payments land in Stripe Clearing.** When Stripe pays out to your bank, record a transfer from Stripe Clearing to your bank account. The clearing account then goes back to zero.
- **Stripe fees appear once they are synced.** The report shows a notice while fees for recent days are still being fetched. Export after it disappears, or the fees for those days will be missing.
- **QuickBooks may not accept journal entries to its own sales tax account.** If your company uses automated sales tax, enter a separate liability account for tax collected.
