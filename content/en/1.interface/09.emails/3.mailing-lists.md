A mailing list is a group of people you send campaigns to. There are two kinds:

- **Subscribers**: people who signed up on your website, or whom you imported or added yourself. They don't need an account at your club.
- **Segment of members**: members who match a filter, such as men over 50, or members with a particular membership. A segment updates itself, and is checked again every time you send to it.

Mailing lists live under **Club Admin → Community → Emails → Mailing lists**.

## Create a list

1. Choose **Create new**.
2. Enter a **Name**, and a **Description** if you want one. The description is shown on the sign-up page.
3. Under **Kind of list**, choose **Subscribers** or **Segment of members**. You can't change this later.
4. Choose **Save**.

From the list's page, **New campaign to this list** starts a campaign with the list already chosen. See [Newsletters and campaigns](/en/interface/emails/newsletters).

## Segments of members

Choose the filter the same way as in a campaign: role, status, membership type, gender and age. The panel underneath shows how many members the segment reaches right now.

A segment has no fixed list of people. Whoever matches the filter when a campaign goes out receives it, so someone who turns 50 next month joins "over 50" next month on their own.

Members of a segment turn emails off by kind, as with any campaign to club members: news and promotions separately.

## Sign-up form on your website

On a subscriber list, turn on **Sign-up form on your website** and save. The list gets a sign-up page on your club's website, and its link appears under the switch. Choose **Copy link** and put it wherever you want people to sign up: your website menu, a news post, social media or a poster's QR code.

People enter their email address, and optionally their name, and agree to receive the emails. They are added only after they confirm:

1. They submit the form and are told to check their inbox.
2. They receive an email asking them to confirm.
3. They open the link and choose **Confirm**.

Until they confirm, they show as **Waiting for confirmation** and receive no campaigns. This stops anyone from signing up someone else's address, and it's your proof that each subscriber agreed.

## Add subscribers yourself

On the list's page, under **Subscribers**:

1. Choose **Add**.
2. Enter one person per line: the email address, then optionally the first name and last name, separated by commas.

   ```text
   ana@example.com, Ana, Horvat
   ivan@example.com
   ```

3. Add **Tags** if you want them on everyone you're adding.
4. Tick **These people agreed to receive emails from the club**.
5. Choose **Add**.

## Import from a spreadsheet

1. Choose **Import CSV**.
2. Drop a CSV file in, up to 5 MB and 20,000 addresses.
3. Add **Tags** if you want them on everyone in the file.
4. Tick **These people agreed to receive emails from the club**.
5. Choose **Import**.

The file needs a column named **email**. Columns named **first name**, **last name** and **tags** are used if they're there. Separate several tags in one cell with `|` or `;`. Files saved by Excel, with commas or semicolons, work as they are. A file without a header row is read as email, first name, last name, tags.

```text
email;first name;last name;tags
ana@example.com;Ana;Horvat;juniors|parents
ivan@example.com;Ivan;;seniors
```

When the import finishes, you see how many people were added, how many were already on the list, and how many were left out.

::warning
Only add or import people who agreed to receive emails from your club. Your confirmation is saved with your name and the date, as the record of their consent.
::

### Who is left out

- **Addresses that aren't valid** are skipped.
- **Someone already on the list** keeps their place. New tags are added, and a missing name is filled in.
- **Someone who unsubscribed** stays unsubscribed. Neither an import nor an admin can put them back. Only they can, by signing up again and confirming.

## Manage subscribers

The subscribers table shows each person's email, name, tags, status and how they joined: **Signed up**, **Imported** or **Added by hand**. Hover over how they joined to see how they agreed. Search by email or name, filter by status, or filter by one tag.

- **Tags**: type a tag into a row and press Enter to add it; select a tag's × to remove it.
- **Unsubscribe**: stops sending them campaigns. They stay in the table as unsubscribed, so they can't be added back by mistake.
- **Delete**: removes the row completely.

## When someone unsubscribes

Every campaign to a subscriber list has a link at the bottom that takes the person off **that list only**. They don't need to sign in. Their row then shows as unsubscribed.

Members who receive a campaign as club members, or through a segment, turn off that kind of email from your club instead. They can change this later in their notification settings.

## Delete a list

Choose the bin icon next to the list in **Mailing lists**. Campaigns that were going to the list will reach nobody, so check scheduled campaigns first.
