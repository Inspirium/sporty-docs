Use Emails to send campaigns and newsletters to your members and subscribers, and to manage the automated (transactional) emails your club sends.

Manage everything from **Club Admin → Community → Emails**.

## Campaigns

The **Emails** list shows every campaign you've sent or drafted, with its subject and send date.

![Email campaign list](/images/emails-list.jpg)

Use **Compose new** to write a new campaign: choose who receives it, write a subject and body, then send it now or schedule it. See [Newsletters and campaigns](/en/interface/emails/newsletters).

## Mailing lists

**Mailing lists** are the people a newsletter goes to. A list holds people who signed up on your website or whom you added yourself, or it's a saved group of members, such as men over 50. See [Mailing lists](/en/interface/emails/mailing-lists).

## Email templates

**Email templates** let you save reusable layouts so campaigns share consistent branding and structure.

## Transactional emails

**Transactional emails** are the automated emails SportyPlus sends on your club's behalf — booking confirmations, receipts, password resets, and similar system messages. You can review and customize their content here.
