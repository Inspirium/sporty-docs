To let some members book courts for free, such as your coaches, staff or a sponsor, give them a membership whose court price is zero. Everyone else keeps paying as before.

It takes three steps:

1. [Create a private membership](#_1-create-the-membership) for them.
2. [Set its court price to zero](#_2-make-the-courts-free) on each court they may book for free.
3. [Give the membership to each member](#_3-give-it-to-members).

If they should get only a few free hours a month, not unlimited free play, see [A monthly allowance instead](#a-monthly-allowance-instead).

## 1. Create the membership

Go to **Club Admin → Community → Memberships → Memberships** and choose **Create new**.

![A new private membership](/images/free-bookings-new-plan.jpg)

1. Give it a **Title** your staff will recognise, such as "Free court play" or "Coaches", and a short **Description**.
2. Switch **Public membership** off. A private plan isn't shown to members, so nobody can buy or ask for it. Only you can give it out.
3. Choose **Save and continue**.

Don't create a sellable item for it: the plan isn't for sale.

On the edit page that opens you can set its other [booking rules](/en/interface/memberships#booking-rules), such as how far ahead its members can book. Choose **Save** when you're done.

## 2. Make the courts free

Court prices are set per membership, in the member app. Open the **App** tab at the top of the admin, then the menu, then **Court management**. For each court the members should book for free:

1. Choose **Prices** on the court's card.
2. Find your new membership in the list and choose **Enter prices** under it.
3. Choose the days. **Select all days** picks the whole week.
4. Choose the hours. Pick every hour the court is open, or only some hours, such as mornings.
5. Enter **0** as the price.
6. Under **Season**, leave every season unselected so the price applies all year. If a season is highlighted, click it to clear it.
7. Choose **Save**.

![A price of zero for the membership](/images/free-bookings-zero-price.jpg)

The membership now shows **0,00 €** for those hours:

![The saved price](/images/free-bookings-court-price.jpg)

Hours you don't price for this membership aren't free. In those hours its members pay the price of your [basic membership](/en/interface/memberships#basic-membership), so you can make only off-peak hours free, for example. To make a court completely free, price every hour it is open.

::warning
If the saved price shows a season name and dates under the time, it only applies during that season. Delete the entry and enter it again with no season selected.
::

## 3. Give it to members

Go to **Club Admin → Community → Members → Member list**, open the member and choose **Edit membership**.

![Giving the membership to a member](/images/free-bookings-assign.jpg)

1. Under **Add new membership**, pick your free plan.
2. Switch on **Unlimited membership**, or set a start and end date, for example for the length of a coach's contract.
3. Choose **Assign membership**.
4. Then choose **Remove** on the member's previous membership, usually your basic one, such as "Guest".

::warning
Remove the old membership. A member who holds two memberships at your club can be charged by either of them, so their bookings may not be free.
::

Repeat this for each member. The change can take up to an hour to show up in the app.

## What members pay

The free membership only covers its holder's share of a booking. When a booking has more players, each one pays according to their own membership. For example, if a coach with the free plan books a court with a guest, the coach's half is free and the guest pays their half.

## Taking it away

Open the member's **Edit membership** and choose **Remove** on the free plan. They go back to your basic membership and pay normal prices again.

## A monthly allowance instead

To give members a set number of free hours each month, rather than free play without limit, leave the court prices as they are and use **Included hours**. On the membership's edit page, under **Booking rules**, click **Included hours**.

![Free hours each month](/images/free-bookings-included-hours.jpg)

1. Pick the number of free hours a month, or type your own under **Hours per month (custom)**.
2. Choose whether unused time is lost at the end of the month (**Reset every month**) or kept (**Carry over unlimited**).
3. Under **Eligible courts**, keep **All courts**, or choose **Selected courts only** and pick them.
4. Choose **Save**.

Members can then pay for a booking with their free hours. Once the hours run out, they pay the normal court price. Give the plan to members the same way, as in [step 3](#_3-give-it-to-members).
