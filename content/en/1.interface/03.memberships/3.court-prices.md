Every membership can have its own court prices. Members might pay 15 € for a slot that costs a guest 26 €, or play cheaper in the mornings. A member pays the price of their own membership. Everyone without a membership pays the price of your [basic membership](/en/interface/memberships#basic-membership).

Prices are set per court, in the member app. Open the **App** tab at the top of the admin, then the menu, then **Court management**. You can also go straight to `/app/admin/courts` on your club's address.

## How prices fit together

- **Start with the basic membership.** Its prices are the base for everyone, and a court can only be booked in hours that have a price. See [Court prices](/en/interface/courts#prices).
- **A membership's prices replace the base price only in the hours you set them.** In every other hour, its members pay the base price. You only need to enter the hours where a membership's price is different.
- **The price is per booking slot.** That is 30, 60 or 90 minutes, depending on the court's **Reservations** setting. A one-hour booking on a 30-minute court costs two slots.

## Entering prices

On **Court management**, choose **Prices** on the court's card. The prices are listed by membership.

![Court prices](/images/courts-prices.jpg)

Choose **Enter prices** under the membership, then go through the steps:

1. **Select days.** Mark the days that share the same price and hours, such as Monday to Friday. **Select all days** marks the whole week. Choose **Next**.

   ![Choosing the days](/images/membership-prices-days.jpg)

2. **Choose a time.** Mark the hours with the same price, such as 5:00 AM to 10:00 AM. **Choose half an hour** lets you mark half hours, for a price that starts or ends on the half hour. Choose **Next**.

   ![Choosing the hours](/images/membership-prices-hours.jpg)

3. **Price.** Enter the price of one booking slot.

   ![Entering the price](/images/membership-prices-price.jpg)

   - **Price on 10.9.2026** appears only for clubs that must show a reference price next to today's price, as Croatian law requires. Enter the price this slot had on that date.
   - **Price by length** is optional. Choose **Add length** to set the total price of a booking by its length, for example 60 minutes for 16 € and 90 minutes for 20 €. Once you add lengths, members can only book those lengths.
   - **Season** — leave every season unselected for a price that applies all year. Select one for a price that applies only in that season (see [Seasonal prices](#seasonal-prices)). Click a selected season again to clear it.

4. Choose **Save**.

The entry appears under the membership, with its days, hours and price:

![The saved price](/images/membership-prices-saved.jpg)

Add as many entries as you need, for example one for weekday mornings and one for weekend evenings. Make sure a membership's entries don't overlap. If two of them cover the same hour, you can't tell which price applies.

Repeat this for each court. To save time, see [Copying prices to another court](#copying-prices-to-another-court).

## Changing or deleting a price

- The pencil on an entry opens the same steps, with the entry's days, hours and price filled in. Change what you need and choose **Save changes**.
- The red cross deletes the entry, after you confirm. **Delete all**, under a membership's entries, deletes all of that membership's prices on this court.

## Seasonal prices

A season, such as Summer or Winter, has its own prices. Choose **Edit seasons** on the prices page to create seasons and set their dates. **Show seasonal prices** filters the list by season.

When a season is running on a court, the court uses only that season's prices, for every membership. Prices without a season don't apply until the season ends. So if a court has summer prices, enter the summer prices for each membership that should pay differently. A membership with no summer prices pays the summer price of its lower membership, or of the basic membership.

::warning
A price saved under a season shows the season's name and dates under its hours. If a season has ended, its prices no longer apply. Check this when a price you entered doesn't show up on the booking page.
::

## Falling back to another membership

By default, in hours where a membership has no price of its own, its members pay the basic membership's price. To fall back to another membership instead, set **Lower membership** on the membership's page, under **Club Admin → Community → Memberships → Memberships**.

![Lower membership](/images/membership-prices-lower.jpg)

- Pick a membership, such as "Senior", and members of this plan pay the Senior price wherever their own plan has none.
- Choose **No lower membership**, and members of this plan can book only the hours their own plan has prices for. Use this for a plan limited to set hours, such as morning-only play.

Choose **Save** at the bottom of the page.

## Copying prices to another court

**Copy prices from another court**, at the top of the prices page, copies another court's opening hours and prices, for every membership, to this court.

::warning
Copying first deletes all prices on this court, for every membership. Use it on a new court, or when both courts should cost exactly the same.
::

## When several people play

Each player pays their share, at their own membership's price. Say a slot costs a member 10 € and a guest 20 €. If a member and a guest book it together, the member pays half of 10 €, which is 5 €, and the guest pays half of 20 €, which is 10 €.
