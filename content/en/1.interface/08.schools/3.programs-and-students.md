A **programme** is what a child is enrolled in and billed for every month: *Basketball school*, *Talent squad*, *Under-10s*. Enrolling a child gives them a **subscription** to the programme, at the programme's price or one you set for that child.

## Creating a programme

A programme is a product in your shop, marked as an academy product:

1. Go to **Shop → New product**.
2. Make it a subscription billed every **Month**, with the monthly price.
3. Set **Product type** to **Academy**.

The **New program** button under **Academy Manager → Programs** also has **Create new product**, which makes one from just a name and a price.

To rename a programme or change its list price, edit the product in **Shop → Products**. A new list price applies to children enrolled from then on; children already enrolled keep their own price until you change it with **Change price**.

## The programmes list

**Academy Manager → Programs**

![Programmes with revenue](/images/academy-programs.jpg)

The top shows active students, monthly revenue, unpaid orders and active programmes. **Overdue payments** lists the programmes with students who owe money. Click a programme to open it.

## Enrolling students

Parents can enrol their children themselves through an [enrolment form](/en/interface/schools/enrolment-forms). To enrol someone yourself:

- **One student:** **Enrol a student** on a programme's page, on **Students**, or on the dashboard. Pick the member, or choose **Not on the list? Add them**. For a child, link a parent who has an account, or create the parent's account too. Then set the price (leave it empty for the programme's price) and the **Status**:
  - **Active — include in monthly billing**: the child is billed from the next order run.
  - **Awaiting first payment**: the child is on the programme but not billed by the monthly run. To make them active later, tick them and choose **Resume**.
- **Many at once:** **Academy Manager → Programs → New program** adds several members to one programme in one go, all active. Despite its name, this page doesn't create a programme; pick an existing one (or create it with **Create new product**), optionally set a price for all of them, add the members, and confirm.

Enrolling never sends the parent anything and doesn't create an order. Orders come from the monthly [billing run](/en/interface/schools/billing).

## A programme's students

Open a programme to see its students with their status, last payment and debt, and the programme's monthly price and revenue. Search by name, email or phone, and filter by status.

Statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | Enrolled and included in the monthly billing run. |
| **Awaiting first payment** | On the programme but not billed by the monthly run. |
| **Past due**, **Unpaid** | Has orders that haven't been paid. |
| **Paused** | Not billed until you resume them. |
| **Canceled** | Removed from the programme. Kept so you can see the history. |

Actions on each student:

- **Move to another program**: moves the student to another programme at that programme's list price. Change the price afterwards if the student had a discount.
- **Change price**: sets the student's monthly price. The next order uses it. Use it for discounts, scholarships or a mid-season change.
- **Send reminder**: for a student with unpaid orders, emails the parent a reminder for each one.
- **Add note**: notes for staff. Parents never see them.
- **Pause** / **Resume**: a paused student is left out of the monthly billing run until you resume them.
- **Remove**: cancels the student's place, after asking. They are no longer billed, and they stay in the list as **Canceled**.

Tick several students to **Pause**, **Resume** or **Remove** them together. **Resume** activates every selected student who isn't active, including students **Awaiting first payment** and **Canceled** ones, so it is also how you bring them back. **Remove** asks before it cancels anyone.

## Students

**Academy Manager → Students** lists every student who has, or has had, a place in any programme, with their parent, programme, price, status, and the month's attendance. A student enrolled in several programmes is shown once, with the most recent one; open the student to see them all.

### A student's page

- **Programmes**: each of the student's programmes, its status, start date and monthly price. **Move to program** asks, then moves the student at the new programme's price. **Remove subscription** cancels the place, after asking; it stays in the history as canceled.
- **Family**: the student's parents and guardians.
- **Training attendances**: the trainings the student was on, by month.
- **Invoices**: the student's orders, with **Send reminder** for unpaid ones and a CSV export. **Generate order** creates one month's order for one programme straight away and emails it to the parent. It refuses a month that already has an unpaid order.
- **Notes**, **Medical documents** and **Equipment**: see [Medical documents and equipment](/en/interface/schools/medical-and-equipment).

**Edit profile** opens the member's profile.

## Questions

**How do sibling discounts work?**
On an enrolment form, the discount is worked out from the form's sibling ladder when you accept the request. When you enrol a child yourself, set the discounted price with **Change price**.

**A family is leaving mid-season. What do I do?**
**Remove** the child from the programme. They won't be in the next billing run. Orders already sent stay as they are; cancel any you don't want to collect under [Billing](/en/interface/schools/billing).
