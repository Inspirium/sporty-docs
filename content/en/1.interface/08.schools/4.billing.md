Every month your school raises one **order** per enrolled student, sends it to the parent, and records the payment. When an order is paid, SportyPlus issues the invoice. If parents pay by bank transfer, importing your bank statement matches the payments for you.

## The monthly run

**Academy Manager → Billing**

### 1. Generate the month's orders

In **Generate orders**, pick the month and year and click **Generate**. SportyPlus creates one order for every student who is **Active** on a programme billed monthly, at that student's own price. Paused, canceled and **Awaiting first payment** students are left out.

The new orders are **Not sent**: nothing has gone to parents yet, so you can check them first. They take a moment to appear.

Generating doesn't happen on its own. The dashboard reminds you when students still need an order for next month.

Generating a month again only adds orders for students who don't have one for that month yet, such as children enrolled since. A cancelled order doesn't count, so cancel a student's order if you want it raised again.

### 2. Send them

When the list shows **{n} unsent orders**, click **Send orders**. Each order is emailed to the student, or for a child to their parents, with a link to pay online. You can also send a single order from its row.

Orders are emailed only if your club has the **Admin order created** email template under [Emails](/en/interface/emails). Reminders use **Admin order reminder**. Without them nothing is sent, and SportyPlus tells you which template to add.

### 3. Record payments

- **Paid online:** the order is marked paid on its own.
- **Paid by bank transfer:** [import your bank statement](#bank-statements), or use **Mark as paid** on the order.
- **Paid in cash:** **Mark as paid**.

Marking an order paid issues its invoice; an **Invoice** badge appears on the row.

### Chasing unpaid orders

Filter by **Pending** and use **Send Reminder** on a row, or select several and **Send Reminders**. A student's page also has **Send reminder** for each unpaid order.

Before chasing anyone, import your latest bank statement: until it's read, an order showing as unpaid may already have been paid. The dashboard warns you when your last import is a week old.

## The orders list

Filter by status (**Not sent**, **Pending**, **Paid**, **Cancelled**), month and year, or search; the list starts on the current month. You can export it to CSV.

On each order:

- **View order** opens it to change lines or amounts.
- **Cancel** cancels it without emailing anyone.
- **Delete** removes a cancelled order.

Selected orders can be deleted together if none of them was ever sent or all were cancelled. SportyPlus asks before deleting, and a deleted order can't be restored.

**Edit invoice design** opens the email and invoice templates.

### One order for one student

On a student's page, **Generate order** creates one month's order for one programme and emails it straight away, for example for a child who joined mid-month. It refuses a month that already has an unpaid order for that programme.

## Bank statements

**Academy Manager → Bank statements**

Import the statement your bank gives you, and SportyPlus matches each incoming payment to an open order.

### Importing

Click **Upload a statement** and choose the file:

- **camt.053 (XML)** is best.
- The bank's **XLS, XLSX or CSV** export also works. Columns are recognised by their headings, in English or Croatian: date, credit (or *uplata*, *potražuje*), amount, payer name, payer IBAN, payment reference (*poziv na broj*) and description.

Files up to 20 MB. The same file can't be imported twice. Only incoming payments are read.

### How payments are matched

| Match | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| The payment uses the order's **payment reference** and the **exact amount** | The order is marked paid and its invoice issued, automatically. |
| The reference matches but the amount differs | Proposed, for you to decide. |
| The payer's bank account was confirmed on an earlier import | Proposed. |
| The description names the student and mentions the order or the month | Proposed. One payment can be proposed against several orders. |
| None of these | Listed under **No suggestion**. |

The counters show how many were **Closed automatically**, how many **Need a decision**, and how many have **No suggestion**.

### Deciding

- **Need a decision:** each proposal says why it was made. **Confirm** attaches the payment to the order; **Not this** rejects the proposal. **Confirm all** confirms every proposal at once, after warning you how many rest only on a name in the description. **Match again** proposes again for everything you haven't confirmed.
- **No suggestion:** search for the open order the payment belongs to and attach it, or choose **Not academy income** for payments that have nothing to do with the school.

A payment that covers what is still owed marks the order paid and issues the invoice. A smaller payment is recorded against the order, which stays open as **partly paid**.

Ask parents to use the payment reference printed on their order. Those payments close on their own.

### Undoing an import

**Discard the last import** removes the most recent statement so you can import it again, for example after uploading the wrong file. It's refused once a payment from it has been confirmed or has paid an order. A payment that has paid an order can't be detached here; that needs a credit note.
