Trainings are bookings on your courts with a coach and students on them. The **Trainers** and **Coach hours** pages are built from those bookings, so there is nothing to log by hand: book the trainings, and the hours follow.

## Schedule

**Academy Manager → Schedule**

The schedule shows your courts side by side. Switch between **Day**, **Detailed** and **Week**, move with the arrows or **Today**, or jump to a date.

### Booking a training

Click a free slot to open **Book training**:

- **Type**: **Academy** for a school group's training, **Private** for an individual lesson. Coach hours are reported separately for the two.
- **Duration**.
- **Repeat**: a number of weekly sessions, or every week until a date. **Every other week** books fortnightly.
- **Trainers**: at least one.
- **Students** and a **Note**.

### Changing or cancelling one

Click a training to change its type, add or remove trainers and students, or cancel it. Cancelling asks you to confirm.

Trainings are not linked to programmes: adding a student to a training doesn't enrol them, and enrolling a student doesn't put them on any training. Add the students to each group's trainings yourself.

## Trainers

**Academy Manager → Trainers** lists, for the month you pick, every coach who is a trainer on at least one training, with their hours and number of sessions. Click a coach to open their hours.

Any member can be picked as a trainer on a training. A coach's public profile on your website is separate, and managed under [Website → Coaches](/en/website/coaches-and-gallery).

## Coach hours

**Academy Manager → Coach hours**

For the month you pick: total coach hours, academy and private hours, the number of trainings and students. The **Academy**, **Private** and **Students** tabs break it down, and each can be exported to CSV.

- **A coach's page** shows their academy hours (the ones usually paid as salary) and private hours (usually charged to the student), their average group size, the days and times they train most, their students, and their trainings for the next 30 days.
- **A student's page** shows the hours the student trained, how many trainings they attended, and with which coaches.

When two coaches share a training, each is counted for the whole training.

SportyPlus doesn't calculate pay: export the hours and use them for payroll however your club does.

## Attendance

A student's [page](/en/interface/schools/programs-and-students#a-students-page) shows the trainings they were on each month, and parents see the same under their child's **Academy** page. In the member app, coaches of a school group record who came.
