The **Academy Manager** runs your club's school: the programmes children train in, who is enrolled, the monthly fees, trainings and coaches' hours, medical certificates and equipment. Find it under **Club Admin → Academy Manager**. It works the same way for any sport.

## How a season runs

1. **Set up your programmes.** Each programme is a monthly fee, such as *Basketball school, €52 a month*. See [Programmes and students](/en/interface/schools/programs-and-students).
2. **Take enrolments.** Publish an [enrolment form](/en/interface/schools/enrolment-forms) and let parents enrol online, or enrol children yourself.
3. **Bill every month.** Generate the month's orders, send them to parents, and record payments. See [Billing and bank statements](/en/interface/schools/billing).
4. **Match payments.** Import your bank statement and let SportyPlus match the payments to orders.
5. **Run trainings.** Book trainings on the schedule; coaches' hours are counted from them. See [Trainings and coaches](/en/interface/schools/trainings-and-coaches).
6. **Keep documents current.** Track medical certificates and lent equipment. See [Medical documents and equipment](/en/interface/schools/medical-and-equipment).

## Dashboard

**Academy Manager → Dashboard** shows what needs doing.

![Academy Manager dashboard](/images/academy-dashboard.jpg)

- **Banners at the top** warn when no bank statement has been imported for a week or more (until you import one, an order showing as unpaid may already be paid), and when students still need an order for next month, with the approximate total.
- **Collected this month**, **Outstanding**, **Active students** and **Collection rate**, each linking to the detail.
- **Needs your attention** lists only what has a count: students without a medical certificate, expired or soon-expiring certificates, bank payments waiting for a decision or without a suggestion, partly paid orders, orders unpaid for over 30 days, equipment past its return date, and programmes with no students.
- **Quick actions**: import a bank statement, prepare orders, enrol a student, add a certificate, send reminders, record a payment, and add students to a programme.

A club with no programmes sees a short setup guide instead.

## What parents see

Each child on a parent's account has an **Academy** page under **My family**. It shows:

- the child's programmes and how far they are paid;
- the month's trainings;
- invoices, with PDFs;
- medical documents, which the parent can upload too;
- equipment the child has borrowed.

Your notes on a student are never shown to parents. Parents can also pay a child's open orders from **My family**. See [Family and children](/en/members/family).

## Coaches on your website

The coach pages on your club website are website content, managed under **Website → Coaches** (see [Coaches and gallery](/en/website/coaches-and-gallery)). They are separate from the **Trainers** and **Coach hours** pages here, which are built from the trainings on your schedule.
