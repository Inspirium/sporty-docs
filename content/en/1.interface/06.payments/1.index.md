SportyPlus takes payments for court bookings, memberships, shop orders, event tickets and academy fees through one payment provider, which you connect once for the whole club.

Setting up payments takes three steps:

1. Fill in your [company details](/en/interface/settings#company-info).
2. Connect a provider under **Business → Payment gateway**.
3. Switch on online payment under **Settings → Payments**.

## Choosing a payment gateway

**Club Admin → Business → Payment gateway**

![Payment gateways](/images/payments-gateways.jpg)

Click a provider to select it. Its settings appear under **Data for selected gateway**.

### Stripe

Stripe is the right choice for most clubs. Members pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, and the money is paid out automatically to your club's bank account.

1. Select **Stripe Express** and choose **Set up Stripe**.
2. Stripe asks for your club's legal details, a representative, and a bank account in the club's name.
3. At the end, choose **Turn on card payments**.

If you stop part-way, **Continue setup** takes you back to where you left off. Once you're connected, **Manage Stripe** shows:

- Your balance and payouts.
- Anything Stripe still needs from you.
- The **Checkout** settings:
  - **Accept card payments at checkout**.
  - **Payment fee** — a percentage added to prices at the final step of payment.
  - **Test mode** — payments go to a test account and no real money moves. Use it to try a booking before you go live.

::note
While Stripe still needs information, such as an ID document for your representative, the overview shows a banner with **Update details**. Card payments or payouts can be paused until you provide it.
::

### Other providers

- **PayFast** — enter the merchant ID, merchant key and passphrase PayFast gave you. Switch on **Sandbox** to test.
- **MiPS** — enter the credentials MiPS issued you. After the first save, the page shows a notification address: give it to MiPS, or payments are never confirmed.
- **Bank transfer** — members pay by bank transfer. Enter the IBAN and account holder shown on the payment slip, and the reference model and prefix used to match payments.
- **E-wallet** — members pay from credit they have topped up at your club.

Each provider has an **Active** switch and a **Save** button.

## Payment settings

**Club Admin → Settings → Payments** decides which ways of paying members are offered.

![Payment settings](/images/settings-payments.jpg)

### Payment options

- **Enable online payment** — members can pay online through your payment gateway.
- **Enable payment on ewallet** — members can pay from their e-wallet balance.
- **Split online payments** — splits online payments between several accounts.
- **Card-only wallet top-ups** — members can top up their wallet only by card, not with cash at the club.
- **Seat holds on open bookings** — every member booking is for 2 or 4 players. Each player who joins has only their own share held on their card, and everyone is charged when the match starts. The player who booked has every other seat held at the guest price, and pays for any seat still empty, and for anyone who joined without paying. Members don't have to add a partner, even if their membership requires one. Shown when online payment is on. See [Bookings for 2 or 4 players](/en/members/booking#bookings-for-2-or-4-players) for what members see.
- **Business invoices** — enables business invoice generation.

### Payment details

Your **Default tax rate**, **Currency** and **Timezone**. They are the same fields as on [Company info](/en/interface/settings#company-info).

### E-wallet top-up surcharge

You can charge a surcharge when members top up their wallet, either a **Percentage** or a **Fixed amount**. To charge it only on larger top-ups, enter an amount under **Only for top-ups of at least**. Leave it empty to charge it on every top-up.

## Orders

**Club Admin → Commercial → Orders** lists every order placed at your club: shop purchases, memberships, event tickets and academy fees.

![Order history](/images/orders-list.jpg)

From an order you can see its details, email a copy, send a payment reminder, or cancel it. The same menu has:

- **Invoices** — invoices issued for paid orders. Set them up under [Settings → Invoices](/en/interface/settings#invoices).
- **Refunds** — refund a paid order.
- **New order** — record an order by hand, such as a payment made at the desk.

For payment totals and card transactions, see [Reports](/en/interface/reports).
