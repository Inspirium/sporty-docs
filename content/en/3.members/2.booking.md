Your club decides which courts you can book, how far ahead, for how long, and what it costs. The app shows you what your membership allows.

## Finding a free court

Open **Court Booking** from the menu or the home page.

- Change the day with the date at the top, the arrows, or by swiping.
- Switch the view between **In detail**, **Day** and **Week**. In the week view choose **Show by days** or **Show by court**.
- Free slots are marked **Available**, taken ones **Booked**. **Show price list** shows the club's price list if it has published one.
- A video icon next to a court means it has a camera for **match recording**.
- If the club has several locations, pick one first.

Prefer to start from the time? **Book a time slot** lists the free times for a day; pick a time, then **Choose a court**.

## Making a booking

1. Tap the free slots you want on a court — each shows its price — then tap **Next**.
2. **Booking options:** add the people you're playing with.
   - **Add a partner to the reservation** searches the club's members; people you've played with recently are listed first.
   - **For couples** lets you book as a doubles pair.
   - **Add guest** adds someone who isn't a member, with their name and email.
   - **Book for the competition** links the booking to a league or tournament match.
   - Some memberships require a partner: the app says **You must add a player to the reservation.**
3. **Confirmation:** check the court, date, time and **Total price**, and how long you can still cancel (**Possibility of cancellation**). You can add a **Remark**, and the club may offer **Products and services** to add, such as match recording.
4. Choose **Book** (or **Book and pay** if your membership requires paying upfront). You'll see **Court booked successfully.**

### Booking rules you may run into

These come from your club and your membership:

- **Length:** courts are booked in 30, 60 or 90-minute slots. You may see **You can't book just 30 minutes.**, **One reservation can be a maximum of 2 hours.**, or a rule that you must book exactly 90 consecutive minutes.
- **No gaps:** some clubs don't allow a booking that leaves a 30-minute gap nobody else can use (**A court booking cannot leave a 30-minute gap.**). Close to the start time this rule may be lifted.
- **How far ahead:** the calendar stops at your membership's limit: **Your membership … allows booking … days in advance.**
- **How many at once:** your membership may limit how many active bookings you can hold; the confirmation shows your current total.
- **Courts members can't book:** some courts are for staff or certain memberships only; you'll see **Reservation not possible**.

### Repeating bookings

If your membership lets you book more than two weeks ahead, the confirmation step offers **Recurring time slot**: repeat **By day** between two dates or **By number of weeks**, **Every week** or **Every other week**. Only slots that are free are booked.

## Paying for a booking

If your club takes payments in the app, you choose how to pay after booking. You'll see only the options your club offers:

- **Card** — pay online by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. If you have a [saved card](/en/members/payments#saved-card) it is used automatically (**Paying with Visa ···· 4242**, with **Change** to use another). A card processing fee may apply; the app tells you if it does.
- **Wallet** — pay from your prepaid balance at the club. Older versions of the app call it **eInvoice**.
- **Credits** — use your membership's free minutes. The app shows how many minutes the booking takes and how many you have.

**Pay later** leaves the booking unpaid; you can pay from **My reservations** with **Pay**. If your membership requires paying upfront, there is no Pay later: a booking that isn't paid within the time the app shows is cancelled automatically.

### Splitting the cost

When booking with others, you may be able to choose **Pay everything** or **Pay your share**. Your partners get a notification with their share. If they don't pay by the deadline, the amount you authorised is charged.

Some clubs also let you leave **open seats** on a booking. Other members can take them from the court calendar, or you can share an invite link with **Invite players**; whoever joins pays their share.

### When a payment fails

- **Your card was declined. Try again or change your card.** — try again or use another card.
- **You don't have enough funds to pay your bill.** — your wallet balance is too low; top it up or pay by card.
- If a payment went wrong and money was taken, use **Report a payment problem** or [Report a problem](/en/members/help).

## Your bookings

**My reservations** (from your picture menu, or the home widget) lists your bookings under **Active** and **Past**. Tap one to open it on the court calendar.

From a booking you can **Add player**, **Send a message** to the other players, and — if your club allows it — **Enter the result** afterwards.

You can't move a booking to another time yourself: cancel it and book again, or ask the club.

## Sync your bookings to your calendar

Your bookings can appear in the calendar app on your phone or computer, and keep themselves up to date there. A new booking shows up, one the club moves is moved, and a cancelled one disappears. Each one has the court, the club's address and who is playing. Bookings you play in or coach are included; past ones stay for 30 days.

Choose **Sync with calendar** in **My reservations** or on the bookings card on your home screen. The app gives you your own private link and three ways to use it:

- **Add to Google Calendar** opens Google Calendar with the link filled in. Confirm with **Add**. This also works for the Google Calendar app on Android, once it has synced.
- **Add to Apple Calendar** opens the Calendar app on an iPhone, iPad or Mac. Confirm with **Subscribe**. Not offered on Android.
- **Copy subscription link** is for Outlook and any other calendar. Paste the link where the calendar lets you add one from the internet. In Outlook, that's **Add calendar → Subscribe from web**.

Your calendar checks for changes on its own schedule, so a change can take a while to appear. Apple Calendar and Outlook let you choose how often in the calendar's settings. Google Calendar decides for itself and can take several hours.

**Keep the link to yourself.** Anyone who has it can see your bookings. If you shared it by mistake, choose **Create a new link**: calendars using the old link stop updating, and you add the new one the same way.

::note
If your calendar shows a **Reconnect your SportyPlus calendar** event, you subscribed with an older link that no longer updates. Remove that calendar, then add your bookings again with **Sync with calendar**.
::

## Cancelling a booking

Choose **Cancel reservation** (or **Cancel** on the booking) and confirm. To cancel several at once, use **Mark all** in **My reservations**.

- Only the person who made the booking can cancel it.
- You can cancel up to the deadline your membership sets. After it, the app says cancellation is no longer possible — contact the club.
- School and training-group sessions can't be cancelled by members.

**What happens to the money:** free minutes go back to your balance; a card payment that the club hasn't collected yet is released on your card; a paid booking is otherwise refunded to your **wallet at the club**, and the app tells you so before you confirm. For any other refund, use [Report a problem](/en/members/help) and choose your club.

## Extras

- **Match recording.** On courts with a camera, the booking may offer **Record your match**. Every player in the booking gets the recording in the app under **My recordings**, where you can download or share it. You can also allow the recording to be used for AI analysis.
- **Door code and lights.** At clubs with access control, the app shows a **Door opening code** after you book, also on the home page. Clubs with remote lights or locks show an **Access Control** box with buttons that work around your booking time.
- **Waiting list.** If a slot is taken and your club allows it, **Notify me if it becomes available.** tells you when it frees up.
- **Weather.** For outdoor courts the calendar warns when rain is likely.
- **Find a partner.** **Find a player!** lets you post an ad for a match — linked to a booking you already have, or as an open ad — and accept the players who sign up.
