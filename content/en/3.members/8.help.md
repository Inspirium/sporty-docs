## Report a problem

**Report a problem** is in your picture menu and the main menu. First choose who it's for:

- **A problem at your club** — a court, the lights or other equipment, a vending machine, or a refund. The club's staff get it.
- **A problem with the app** — a payment, a wrong score or standings, a booking that didn't work, signing in, or something not working. The SportyPlus team gets it.

Pick what it's about, describe what happened, and add up to five photos. For problems with the app, a screenshot of the screen you were on is attached automatically; you can remove it. Choose **Send**. You'll get an answer by email, and **Reported problems** in your menu shows the status of each report.

Some screens have a shortcut: **Report a wrong score** on a match result, and **Report a payment problem** after a failed payment.

## Common questions

**How do I cancel a booking?**
Open it from **My reservations** and choose **Cancel reservation**. You can cancel up to the deadline your membership sets; only the person who booked can cancel. See [Cancelling a booking](/en/members/booking#cancelling-a-booking).

**Where does my money go when I cancel?**
Free minutes go back to your balance. A card payment the club hasn't collected yet is released. Otherwise the amount goes to your wallet at the club. See [Cancelling a booking](/en/members/booking#cancelling-a-booking).

**Do I get a refund if bad weather stopped play?**
Refunds for rain or other bad weather are decided by the club where you booked, not by SportyPlus. Contact that club — use [Report a problem](#report-a-problem) and choose your club, or find its contact details under **About the club**.

**Why can't I book this court or time?**
Your club and your membership decide which courts you can book, how far ahead, for how long and how many at once. The app says which rule applies. See [Booking rules](/en/members/booking#booking-rules-you-may-run-into).

**How do I pay by card?**
After booking, choose **Card**. Save a card under **Connected accounts** to use it at every club. See [Paying for a booking](/en/members/booking#paying-for-a-booking).

**What is the wallet (eInvoice)?**
A prepaid balance at your club that you top up and pay from. See [Wallet](/en/members/payments#wallet).

**How do I join a club or buy a membership?**
See [Joining a club](/en/members#joining-a-club) and [Memberships](/en/members/payments#memberships).

**How do I sign up for a league or tournament?**
See [Leagues](/en/members/competitions#leagues).

**My result is wrong.**
If it isn't confirmed yet, the other side can dispute it. If it is confirmed, choose **Report a wrong score** on the result.

**I forgot my password.**
Choose **Forgot your password?** on the login page. See [Your account](/en/members/account).

**How do I add my child?**
**My family** → **Add a child**. See [Family and children](/en/members/family).

**How do I delete my account?**
**Edit profile** → **Permanently delete profile**. See [Your account](/en/members/account#deleting-your-account).

**Who decides prices, opening hours and cancellation rules?**
Your club. **About the club** shows its contact details and rules.

## Contact

For questions about your club — prices, memberships, refunds, groups — contact the club. For anything about SportyPlus itself, write to [info@sporty.plus](mailto:info@sporty.plus).
