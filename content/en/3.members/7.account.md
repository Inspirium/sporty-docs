Your SportyPlus account is one account for every club: the same login works at all of them.

## Editing your profile

Your picture menu → **Edit profile**. You can change your photo, name, email, sex, date of birth, country, town, address and phone number. **Save changes** appears once you've changed something.

Change the app's language from the flag in the menu (**Change language**), or on the login page.

## Logging in and passwords

- **Forgot your password?** on the login page emails you a link to set a new one. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.
- To change your password while signed in: **Edit profile** → **Change password**.
- If you signed up with Apple, Google or Microsoft and have no password yet, **Connected accounts** offers **Set a password**.

### Passkeys

A passkey lets you sign in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN instead of a password. Add one under your picture menu → **Sign-in and security** → **Passkeys** → **Add a passkey**. **Sign in with a passkey** is available in the phone apps and on my.sporty.plus; on a club's own web address the app sends you to my.sporty.plus to add one.

### Two-factor sign-in

Under **Sign-in and security** → **Authenticator app** → **Set up**, scan the code with an authenticator app, enter the code, and choose **Turn on**. Save the recovery codes it shows you. From then on the app asks for a code after your password; if you don't have your phone, use **Use a recovery code**.

### Logging out

Your picture menu → **Log out**.

## Connected accounts

Your picture menu → **Connected accounts** shows:

- the Apple, Google or Microsoft accounts you can sign in with — **Connect** or **Disconnect**;
- your [DUPR](/en/members/competitions#dupr) account;
- your [saved card](/en/members/payments#saved-card).

## Notifications

**Notifications** → **Edit** lets you switch each type of notification on or off, separately for push and email and per club. **Reset to default** undoes your changes. Invoices, payment requests and password resets are always emailed.

## Privacy

**Edit profile** → **Privacy settings** decides whether your profile is public and whether others can see your name, email, phone and date of birth. Set it for all clubs, or use **Customize for this club**.

## Leaving a club

Tap the **×** next to the club in the menu, or **Edit profile** → **List of clubs you are a member of** → **Unsubscribe**. Your account stays; you're just no longer a member of that club.

## Deleting your account

**Edit profile** → **Permanently delete profile**, then confirm. This removes your SportyPlus profile for every club.
