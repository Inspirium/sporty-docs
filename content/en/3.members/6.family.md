**My family** in your picture menu is where you manage your children and your partner.

## Adding a child

- **Add a child** creates a profile you manage: first name, last name, date of birth and sex, and optionally an email. Without an email, you manage the profile and the child can't sign in.
- **Link an existing account** connects to a child who already has an account: enter their email and choose **Send request**. They accept it in their own app.

Children appear with **Managed by you** or **Waiting for approval**. From each child you can open their **Academy** page, edit their details, or remove them.

### The age of 16

Children under 16 can't create an account on their own: the sign-up form asks for a parent or guardian's email, and the account opens once the guardian approves it. As a parent you can edit a child's profile while you manage it or while they are under 16.

## Linking your partner

**Link your partner** connects two adult accounts. You can then buy for each other and share a family membership.

Requests from others — to be a child's parent or guardian, or your partner — appear on the same page with **Accept** and **Reject**.

## Paying for your family

- **Buying for someone:** when you buy a membership or a shop item, choose the family member under **For**. A family membership lets you tick everyone it covers.
- **Paying a child's orders:** the **To pay** section lists your children's unpaid orders. Choose **Pay**, pick whose name the invoice is made out to — yours or the child's — and pay by card or from your wallet. The order stays the child's.
- **Handing over a purchase:** under **My orders**, **Who is it for?** assigns a membership or ticket you bought to yourself, a child or your partner.

## A child's page

Open a child to see:

- **Programs** they're enrolled in and **Paid until**;
- their **Trainings**;
- their invoices, paid and unpaid;
- **Medical documents** — upload a medical certificate, vaccination record or sports physical;
- **Equipment**.

## Enrolling a child in a school

If your club's school takes enrolments online, it publishes a link or a QR code to its **enrolment form** on the club website.

1. Open the link and sign in, or create an account from the form.
2. Choose the **programme**, then one of your children or **Add a child**.
3. Fill in the child's details, your phone, and optionally the second parent's contacts, then answer the school's questions.
4. Read each consent and tick the ones you agree to. Required ones must be ticked to enrol; optional ones, such as photos and video, you can leave unticked.
5. Type your full name to sign, and send the enrolment.

If a brother or sister is already in the school and the school gives a sibling discount, the form shows the price before you sign. You get an email with a copy of everything you agreed to, then another once the school accepts or declines the enrolment.

**My enrolments** in your picture menu lists your enrolments and where each one stands. You can **Withdraw** one the school hasn't decided on yet. Once the school accepts, the child's monthly fees appear under **To pay** like any other order.

## School and academy groups

Coaches and the club put players into school or academy groups. If your club has no enrolment form, contact the club to join a group.

If you or your child is in a group, its page shows the coach, the **Training schedule**, **Group messages** and **Group members**. **My profile** in the group shows your attendance (**Arrivals**), payments, and the coach's notes and reports. Attendance is recorded by the coach.
