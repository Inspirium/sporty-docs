People trust a sign-in button they recognise. Use one of these instead of drawing your own.

![The Sign in with Sporty button in light, dark and blue, each with an icon-only version](/images/sign-in-with-sporty-buttons.png)

The Sporty mark on its own is [mark.svg](/brand/sign-in-with-sporty/mark.svg), for building a
button in your own component library.

## Use it as HTML

Link the image to your application's own sign-in route, the one that starts the
[authorization code flow](/en/oauth/authorization-code):

```html
<a href="/auth/sporty" aria-label="Sign in with Sporty">
  <img src="https://docs.sporty.plus/brand/sign-in-with-sporty/light.svg"
       width="240" height="44" alt="Sign in with Sporty">
</a>
```

Host a copy of the SVG yourself if you prefer. The files will not change under you.

To build your own button, keep to the same parts: the mark on the left, the words **Sign in
with Sporty**, white `#FFFFFF`, dark `#131314` or Sporty blue `#02AAF5` as the background, and
corners rounded about as much as your other buttons.

## Rules

- Say **Sign in with Sporty** or **Continue with Sporty**, or the same in your site's
  language. Do not shorten it to "Sporty" on a full-width button.
- Do not recolour, stretch or redraw the mark.
- Give it the same size and weight as the other sign-in buttons beside it. Do not make it
  smaller than 40 pixels tall.
- Keep space around it, about half its height.
- The button starts a sign-in and nothing else. Do not use it for a link to Sporty.
