## Coaches

**Website → Coaches** showcases the coaches at your club on the public site — name, photo, bio, and specialties. This is separate from the [Academy Manager trainer accounts](/interface/schools#students-and-trainers), which handle scheduling and payroll; the Coaches module is purely the public-facing profile.

## Gallery

**Website → Gallery** manages photo albums shown on your website. Toggle the module on, then upload images and organize them into albums under **Gallery → Settings**.

## Video

**Website → Video** lets you feature a video on your homepage — for example a club highlight reel or promotional video.

Like every content module, Coaches, Gallery, and Video each have a **Module activated** toggle and a live preview, matching the pattern used by [News](/website/news-and-events) and [Shop](/interface/shop).
