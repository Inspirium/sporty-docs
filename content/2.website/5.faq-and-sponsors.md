## FAQ

**Website → FAQ** manages a frequently-asked-questions section for your public website — a simple way to answer common questions about booking, membership, or club policies without members having to contact you.

## Sponsors

**Website → Sponsors** lets you showcase sponsor or partner logos on your website. Add a sponsor's name, logo, and optional link — useful for clubs that run on sponsorship support.

Both modules follow the standard content-module pattern: a **Module activated** toggle to show or hide the section, and simple fields to manage its content.
