## Hero

The **Hero** module is the banner at the top of your homepage — your club name, logo, a welcome message, and a call-to-action button. Edit it under **Website → Hero**; the preview updates live as you type.

![Hero module preview](/images/website-hero-preview.jpg)

## Pages

**Website → Pages** manages custom content pages — an "About us" page, a sport explainer, a community page, and so on.

![Pages list](/images/website-pages-list.jpg)

Each page can be published or left as a draft, and once published it can be added to the [navigation menu](/website#navigation) or linked as your [Terms and conditions / Privacy policy](/interface/settings#legal).

## CTA

The **CTA** (call-to-action) module is a banner section you can place anywhere on the homepage to drive visitors toward a specific action, such as signing up or booking a court.

## Footer

**Website → Footer** controls the content shown at the bottom of every page — contact info, social links, and any custom footer text.
