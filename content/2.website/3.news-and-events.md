## News

**Website → News** is your club's blog/announcements feed. Toggle the module on to show it on your website, then use **Create new** to publish a post.

![News list](/images/website-news-list.jpg)

## Events

**Website → Events** lists everything happening at your club — competitions, socials, junior-club activities, maintenance notices, and more, organized into categories.

![Events module with category filters](/images/website-events-module.jpg)

Use **Create new event** to publish one. Each event can have its own buyers list and waitlist for ticketed events (see the public `/events` pages).
