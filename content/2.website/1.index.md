Every SportyPlus club gets a public website built from content modules — Hero, Pages, News, Events, Coaches, Gallery, and more. Turn modules on or off, reorder them, and edit their content without touching code.

Manage your website from the **Website** tab in the admin sidebar switcher (next to **Club Admin** and **Super Admin**).

## Modules

**Website → Modules** is where you decide which sections appear on your homepage, and in what order. Drag active modules to reorder them, or add one from the inactive list on the right.

![Website modules manager](/images/website-modules.jpg)

Each module (Shop, Courts, Features, Events, Gallery, Video, Memberships, News, Coaches, FAQ, CTA...) also has its own settings page where you toggle it independently and edit its content — see the pages below.

## Navigation

**Website → Navigation** controls the links shown in your site's main menu. Add any content module or published page to the menu, and reorder items by dragging.

![Website navigation editor](/images/website-navigation.jpg)

## Styles

**Website → Styles** lets you customize the visual theme of your public website — colors and branding — without editing code.
